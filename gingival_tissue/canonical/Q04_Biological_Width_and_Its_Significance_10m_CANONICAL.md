## 1. Biological width and its significance

### Explanation

Definition and seal: "combined vertical dimension of the junctional epithelium and the supra-alveolar connective tissue", which "acts as a seal around the cervical portion of the tooth" and "reattaches to the original level in approximately 5 days" after separation (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC]; Summitt's uses the same definition via the dentogingival junction (Fruits et al, 2013) [SRC]. Gargiulo 2.04 mm (sulcus 0.69, JE 0.97, CT 1.07); Vacek 1.91 mm (sulcus 1.32, JE 1.14, CT 0.77); CT most consistent, JE most variable, wider posteriorly (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC]. Dentogingival complex 3.0 mm facially, 3.0-4.5 mm proximally; bone sounding; 3.0 mm in 85% of subjects allows a margin 0.5 mm subgingival (Fruits et al, 2013) [SRC]. Normal crest on 85% of anterior teeth; high crest (2%) poses "the greatest risk for violation of biologic width" (Rouse, 2013) [SRC]. Violation: bacterial colonisation, inflammation, attachment loss, recession or pocket; width re-forms apically; thin tissue recedes, thick tissue forms a moat-like defect (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC]; intrasulcular margins doubled bleeding risk (Fruits et al, 2013) [SRC]. Margin limits differ: Ricketts 0.5 mm into sulcus (Ricketts, 2011) [SRC]; Sturdevant's no more than 1 mm into sulcus, 3 mm minimum to bone, aim 5 mm surgically (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC]; Summitt's 2.5 mm (Fruits et al, 2013) [SRC]; Sikri 2.0 mm (Sikri, 2017) [SRC]. Correction: crown lengthening (bone removal with soft tissue repositioning), orthodontic extrusion in the aesthetic zone (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC]; provisional observed for several weeks (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) [SRC].

#### ✎ Exam core

**Definition.** Biological width is the combined vertical dimension of the junctional epithelium and the supra-alveolar connective tissue attachment, coronal to the alveolar crest. It seals the cervical tooth against bacteria and repairs itself: detached epithelial attachment re-forms in about 5 days (Morelli and Neiva, 2019).

**Dimensions** (Morelli and Neiva, 2019):

| mm | Gargiulo 1961 | Vacek 1994 |
|---|---|---|
| Sulcus | 0.69 | 1.32 |
| Junctional epithelium | 0.97 | 1.14 |
| Connective tissue | 1.07 | 0.77 |
| Biologic width | 2.04 | 1.91 |

Connective tissue is the most constant; junctional epithelium most variable; width greater posteriorly. Dentogingival complex (sulcus + biologic width) is about 3 mm facially and 3-4.5 mm proximally (Fruits et al, 2013).

**Significance during restorative procedures**
1. **Diagnosis by bone sounding:** a 3 mm reading (85% of subjects) permits a margin 0.5 mm subgingival; under 3 mm suggests a high crest with greatest risk of violation (Fruits et al, 2013; Rouse, 2013).
2. **Margin placement:** supragingival preferred; subgingival margins no deeper than 0.5 mm (Ricketts, 2011) or 1 mm (Morelli and Neiva, 2019) into the sulcus. Minimum margin-to-crest distance differs: 2.0 mm (Sikri, 2017), 2.5 mm (Fruits et al, 2013), 3 mm (Morelli and Neiva, 2019).
3. **Consequences of violation:** bacterial colonisation, chronic inflammation, bleeding, attachment loss and bone resorption as the width re-forms apically; recession in thin tissue, pockets and moat-like bony defects in thick tissue (Morelli and Neiva, 2019). Intrasulcular margins doubled bleeding risk (Fruits et al, 2013).
4. **Correction:** surgical crown lengthening (bone removal with soft tissue repositioning) to give at least 3 mm; orthodontic extrusion for single teeth in the aesthetic zone (Morelli and Neiva, 2019).
5. **Provisional restorations:** well-adapted provisionals, with tissue health observed for several weeks, confirm no violation before final impression (Morelli and Neiva, 2019).
6. **Impressions:** retraction must not tear the attachment; with high or low crests keep retraction minimal and margins at or above the gingiva where possible.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- BW = JE + CT (not sulcus); 2.04 (Gargiulo), 1.91 mm (Vacek).
- Complex 3 mm facial, 3-4.5 mm proximal.
- Margin-to-crest: Sikri 2.0, Summitt 2.5, Sturdevant 3 mm.
- Correct by crown lengthening or orthodontic extrusion.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES

- Giving one universal margin rule without naming the source.
- Forgetting the consequences and correction, which carry the "significance" marks.
