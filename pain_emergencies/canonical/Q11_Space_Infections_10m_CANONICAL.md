# Space Infections

**Marks:** 10 · Short essay

**Answer blueprint:** Definition and spread → Spaces involved → Clinical features → Management → Complications

## Definition and spread

Fascial spaces are potential spaces between fascia and organs that open when pus spreads; an apical abscess may evolve to cellulitis and reach them (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021). Spread follows the root apex's relation to the cortical plate and muscle attachment (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021). Three patterns occur: abscess, cellulitis and necrotizing fasciitis (Brahim and Ord, 2017).

## Spaces involved

| Space | Source and rule (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021) |
|---|---|
| Sublingual | Mandibular tooth, lingual plate perforated, apex above mylohyoid |
| Submandibular | Usually a molar, apex below mylohyoid |
| Submental | Anterior tooth, lingual perforation, apex below mylohyoid |
| Buccal | Posterior tooth, apex above buccinator (maxilla) or below it (mandible) |
| Pterygomandibular | Mandibular second or third molar |
| Canine (infraorbital) | Maxillary canine or first premolar, apex above levator anguli oris |

Secondary spread reaches the lateral pharyngeal, retropharyngeal and danger spaces (Brahim and Ord, 2017).

## Clinical features

- **Abscess:** localised, painful, fluctuant swelling; **cellulitis:** diffuse, firm, brawny swelling (Brahim and Ord, 2017).
- Fever, chills, lymphadenopathy, headache and nausea (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- Trismus; lateral pharyngeal swelling shows as pharyngeal bulge and uvular deviation (Brahim and Ord, 2017).
- **Danger signs:** dysphagia, odynophagia, muffled "hot potato" voice, drooling (Brahim and Ord, 2017).

## Management

Principles are airway, removal of the source, dependent drainage, culture, drain placement and antibiotics (Brahim and Ord, 2017).

- **Airway:** secure it; refer patients with limited opening, difficulty swallowing or systemic signs to an oral surgeon for extraoral drainage and intravenous antibiotics (Walton and Ruparel, 2021).
- **Source:** remove it by debridement or extraction (Walton and Ruparel, 2021).
- **Drainage:** incise and drain, widely in cellulitis (Brahim and Ord, 2017).
- **Antibiotics:** systemic for cellulitis (Walton and Ruparel, 2021), empirically a penicillin, clindamycin or metronidazole (Brahim and Ord, 2017).

## Complications

- **Ludwig angina:** submental, sublingual and submandibular spaces involved together, with airway obstruction (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021); it requires immediate hospitalization (Walton and Hutter, 2002); penicillin cut mortality from 54% to 4% (Brahim and Ord, 2017).
- **Cavernous sinus thrombosis** from the canine space through valveless veins (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- **Mediastinitis:** 71% of mediastinal spread came from the retrovisceral space (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- Brain abscess, septic shock and necrotizing fasciitis (Brahim and Ord, 2017).
