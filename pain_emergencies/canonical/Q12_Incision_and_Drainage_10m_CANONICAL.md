# Incision and drainage

**Marks:** 10 · Short essay

**Answer blueprint:** Definition and rationale → Indications → Anaesthesia → Technique → Drain and aftercare → Endodontic context

## Definition and rationale

An incision gives pus a pathway out and prevents spread of abscess or cellulitis (Baumgartner and Rosenberg, 2019). It decompresses oedema, often with significant pain relief (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).

## Indications

- A swelling that has "pointed" into a soft, fluctuant mass (Gopikrishna, 2021).
- Cellulitis, whether indurated or fluctuant (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- A separate abscess not communicating with the apex, drained through both tooth and mucosa (Walton and Ruparel, 2021).

Books differ on a hard, indurated swelling:

| Author | Position |
|---|---|
| (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021) | Incise it |
| (Gopikrishna, 2021) | Warm saline for 5 minutes hourly until fluctuant; some incise when painful |
| (Sikri, 2019) | Do not incise a non-fluctuant swelling; it may spread the infection |

## Anaesthesia

Local anaesthetic is frequently ineffective in acidic inflamed tissue, and a needle forced into the swelling may spread infection (Gopikrishna, 2021). Use a refrigerant spray after drying the mucosa, or a block with infiltration around, not in, the swelling (Gopikrishna, 2021); Sikri (2019) prefers block.

## Technique

1. If a culture is needed, aspirate first (Baumgartner and Rosenberg, 2019). Place a vertically oriented stab incision, with a No. 11 scalpel through the periosteum, at the most dependent site of the swelling (Gopikrishna, 2021; Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
2. Dissect bluntly with a hemostat or periosteal elevator to open pockets of exudate (Baumgartner and Rosenberg, 2019). Compress the tissue to express pus (Gopikrishna, 2021) and irrigate with saline or 0.12% chlorhexidine (Walton and Ruparel, 2021).
3. Place a drain.

## Drain and aftercare

- **Drain:** dental dam, Penrose or capillary, non-latex if allergic, sutured except in very small swellings (Baumgartner and Rosenberg, 2019); half-inch iodoform gauze is an alternative (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021). A completely evacuated localised swelling may need none (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- **Removal and rinses:** drain out 1 or 2 days after improvement; warm salt-water rinses (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
- **Follow-up:** daily in cellulitis (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021); procedural pain resolves in 2 to 3 days (Walton and Ruparel, 2021).

## Endodontic context

Incision follows debridement of the canal for periradicular swelling (Baumgartner and Rosenberg, 2019); finish endodontic treatment soon after (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021). Disocclude an extruded tooth slightly and prescribe analgesics and antibiotics as needed (Gopikrishna, 2021). Refer extraoral drainage (Teixeira and Cheung, 2021).
