## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none used. The sentence 'Systemic drugs fail in the necrotic canal; topical agents act there' is reasoning drawn from Teixeira and Cheung (no circulation in necrotic pulp) and Sikri (Ledermix acts through dentinal tubules).
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** 'Cohen's chapter does not recommend antibiotics after endodontic surgery' paraphrases Teixeira and Cheung (list of situations in which antibiotics are not recommended). Reiser's sign-based wording is shown beside it as a difference, not resolved.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) duration: 7 days (Baumgartner and Rosenberg), 5-7 days (Gopikrishna), 3 to 4 days (Segura-Egea and Martín-González), 1 or 2 days beyond resolution (Teixeira and Cheung); (2) amoxicillin schedule: loading 1,000 mg then 500 mg 8-hourly vs Sikri 500 mg thrice a day (no loading dose); amoxicillin with clavulanate 1,000 then 500 8-hourly (Baumgartner and Rosenberg) vs 625 mg 12-hourly (Gopikrishna); (3) drug of choice: penicillin (Sikri) vs amoxicillin (Teixeira and Cheung; Gopikrishna); (4) fever threshold above 100°F (Baumgartner and Rosenberg; Teixeira and Cheung) vs above 38° C (Segura-Egea and Martín-González) vs 101°F (Reiser); (5) after endodontic surgery: not recommended (Teixeira and Cheung) vs sign-based (Reiser).
