## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none used.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the six-row space table selects six of Cohen's twelve-plus spaces on the usual exam list; each row is a close paraphrase of Cohen's source rule. 'Secure the airway' as step 1 follows Brahim and Ord's Box 10.1 ('Establish airway' first).
- **Conflicting sources:** none material. Cohen groups spaces after Hohl et al. (mandible and below; cheek and lateral face; pharyngeal and cervical; midface) whereas Brahim and Ord speak of primary and secondary spaces: different classifications, not contradictory. Cohen defines Ludwig angina as submental + sublingual + submandibular involvement; Brahim and Ord as bilateral involvement of the same three spaces.
- **Numerical claims:** 54% to 4% (q11_mortality, Brahim and Ord); 71% (q11_mediastinum, Teixeira and Cheung, a retrospective study cited there).
