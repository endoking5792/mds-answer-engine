## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** the closing sentence of the conclusion and the instruction to exclude referred pain before treating (management step 1) are reasoning, not cited facts; the table row heads in the Aδ/C table are a synthesis of Diogenes and Henry (fibre-pain match) and Ruparel and Khan (distribution).
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the stage sequence transduction-transmission-modulation-perception is a standard pain-physiology frame used for structure; the book passages support each stage individually.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) dull ache: Diogenes and Henry attribute pulpitic dull aching pain to C fibres, Ruparel and Khan say it may also be Aδ fibres that lost myelin. (2) throbbing: Diogenes and Henry say synchronous firing with the heartbeat; Ruparel and Khan report a study finding no synchrony with the arterial pulse. (3) relief by cold: Diogenes and Henry (lowered nociceptor threshold) versus Sikri (gas contraction hypothesis). (4) pretreatment ibuprofen dose: 400 mg in Cohen versus 600 mg in Sikri (Sikri's text, same chapter lists flurbiprofen 100 mg). Plain slip: Sikri Ch.3 spells the nucleus 'subnucleus candalis' (caudalis); the answer uses caudalis. Terminology: Sikri uses 'hot tooth' and 'central core theory' for anaesthetic failure; not needed here.
