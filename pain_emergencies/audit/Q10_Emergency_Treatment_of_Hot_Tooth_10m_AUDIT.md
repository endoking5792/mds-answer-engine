## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** The sequence confirm anaesthesia -> supplemental technique -> pulp removal -> drugs is the drafter's order, assembled from Torabinejad Ch.9 (cold test before access), Cohen Ch.6 (supplemental injections) and Grossman Ch.7 (management list). 'Reassure' follows Walton and Ruparel's patient management emphasis. The row on pulpotomy with MTA or calcium hydroxide in an open apex is Sikri's statement for irreversible pulpitis.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) Buccal articaine infiltration: Grossman calls 4% articaine buccal infiltration (0.9-1.2 mL) one of the most effective supplements to an IANB; Cohen says articaine infiltration in endodontically involved mandibular teeth does not give predictable pulpal anaesthesia and prefers IO and IL; Torabinejad Ch.8 also says buccal articaine infiltration of mandibular teeth after an IANB gives anaesthesia in general. Shown side by side without ranking. (2) Buffering: Grossman says a quicker onset in a hot tooth; Torabinejad Ch.8 says buffering did not statistically improve IANB success in symptomatic irreversible pulpitis. Both stated. (3) Partial pulpectomy vs pulpotomy: Torabinejad Ch.9 reports >90% success for both but discourages partial pulpectomy in severely inflamed teeth; Cohen Ch.19 advocates pulpotomy for time constraints. (4) Premedication: Cohen Ch.6 says NSAID pretreatment reduces postoperative pain; Torabinejad Ch.8 says analgesics 60 minutes before the block give no significant advantage for block success. Not a conflict (different outcomes) and stated as such.
- **Numerical claims:** five times (Grossman); 0.9-1.2 mL articaine (Grossman); 0.2 mL IL (Grossman); 50-96% IL success and 23 minutes (Cohen); about 90% IO (Cohen); 15-20 minutes IP (Cohen); 30-50% nitrous oxide (Torabinejad Ch.8); over 90% (Torabinejad Ch.9); 400 mg ibuprofen, 100 mg flurbiprofen, 1000 mg acetaminophen, 30 minutes (Cohen); 600 mg ibuprofen (Torabinejad Ch.9).
