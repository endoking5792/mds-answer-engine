## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator, except the items under Interpretation.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** The question is case-based and the books have no chapter on a flare-up in a child. The answer applies the general flare-up management (Walton and Ruparel; Teixeira and Cheung; Gopikrishna) to the immature-tooth material (Diogenes, Botero and Kang; Diogenes, Simon and Law; Paranjpe and Noblett; Cvek). The assumption is that the incisor is an immature tooth with pulp necrosis, probably after trauma, flaring during treatment (for example between visits of an apexification or regenerative procedure). That assumption, the link between 'flare-ups occur mostly in necrotic teeth' and this case, the advice to infiltrate away from the swelling in a maxillary incisor (Grossman gives the principle, Cohen the maxillary anterior IL injection caution), and the statement that a child's antibiotic dose must be set by weight (inferred from Grossman's table headed 'Oral Dosage in Adults') are reasoning. The books give no paediatric doses in the passages used; none are quoted.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) Antibiotics in an immature tooth with swelling: Cvek (Andreasen 2007) lists antibiotics for 7 days in acute periapical osteitis and swelling in luxated or replanted immature teeth before calcium hydroxide filling; Teixeira and Cheung and Walton and Ruparel restrict antibiotics to systemic signs, cellulitis, trismus, progressive swelling, and say they are not necessary for localized swelling. Shown side by side; Cvek's protocol is older and for luxated teeth. (2) Time to second regenerative visit: Diogenes, Botero and Kang 2-4 weeks; Diogenes, Simon and Law 3-4 weeks. (3) Intracanal medicament in regeneration: Diogenes, Simon and Law favour calcium hydroxide or an antibiotic paste at 1-10 mg/mL and warn of minocycline staining; Diogenes, Botero and Kang list calcium hydroxide or triple antibiotic paste. (4) Maxillary anterior flare-up frequency: Teixeira and Cheung say maxillary lateral incisors; no other book repeats this.
- **Numerical claims:** 2 to 3 years (Diogenes, Botero and Kang); 4 to 6 weeks (Paranjpe and Noblett); 1.5% NaOCl (Diogenes, Botero and Kang); 5 mm MTA plug; over 90% success; 18 months calcium hydroxide (Paranjpe and Noblett); 2-4 and 3-4 weeks; 6 and 12 months; 12-18 months recall; 7 days antibiotics (Cvek).
