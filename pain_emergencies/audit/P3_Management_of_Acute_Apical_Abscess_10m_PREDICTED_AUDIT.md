## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** The stage-wise table is built from Rôças and Siqueira's initial/intermediate/advanced stages, Sikri's four swelling categories and Walton and Ruparel's three necrosis categories, collapsed to four rows. The Phoenix section combines the definition (Grossman Ch.5), the hypothesis (Sikri Ch.23) and Sikri's management and prevention lists. The statement that 'management follows the stage above' for a Phoenix abscess is the drafter's synthesis; Sikri's own Phoenix treatment lists corticosteroid-antibiotic paste where Walton and Ruparel and Rôças and Siqueira use calcium hydroxide, so the answer says 'an intracanal medicament' without naming the paste.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) Indurated swelling: Walton and Ruparel incise fluctuant or non-fluctuant localized swelling; Sikri advises against incising a non-fluctuant swelling; Rôças and Siqueira say there is no solid evidence either way; Gopikrishna advises warm saline rinses until it softens, noting that some clinicians incise hard tissue anyway. Shown in the table. (2) Amoxicillin: Walton and Ruparel 1 g load, then 500 mg every 6 hours for 2 to 3 days; Gopikrishna 1000 mg load, then 500 mg every 8 hours for 5-7 days (Walton and Ruparel add that shorter courses are as efficacious). Shown side by side. (3) Anaesthesia: Sikri says block anaesthesia can be given for psychological reasons and is preferred when draining through the canal; Gopikrishna says anaesthesia is not routinely needed and injection should avoid the swollen tissue; the answer follows Gopikrishna. (4) Terminology: Sikri Ch.23 equates the Phoenix abscess with mid-treatment flare-up; Grossman Ch.5 defines it as an exacerbation of an existing asymptomatic apical periodontitis; the answer uses Grossman's definition and Sikri's hypothesis.
- **Numerical claims:** 15 to 30 minutes (TEI); files up to #25 (Walton and Ruparel); 1 g load and 500 mg every 6 hours for 2 to 3 days (Walton and Ruparel); 1000 mg and 500 mg every 8 hours for 5-7 days (Grossman Table 7.2). Pages are in the ledger above.
