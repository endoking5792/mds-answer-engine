## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none used.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** step order in the Technique list merges Grossman (stab incision, compression), Teixeira and Cheung (vertical orientation) and Baumgartner and Rosenberg (blunt dissection); 'Where culture is needed, aspirate before incising' follows Baumgartner and Rosenberg's sampling paragraph.
- **Conflicting sources:** indurated (non-fluctuant) swelling: Teixeira and Cheung incise it (indicated whether indurated or fluctuant); Gopikrishna bathes it in warm saline 5 minutes hourly until fluctuant while noting some clinicians incise hard tissue when pain is present; Sikri says not to incise a localised non-fluctuant swelling.
