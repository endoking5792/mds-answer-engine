## STUDY MODE

### Principles
- All sealers are cytotoxic when freshly mixed and substantially less toxic once set (Berman et al, 2021, via digest; Gatewood, 2007).
- Cell-culture models lack lymphatic drainage and defence cells (polymorphonuclear leukocytes, plasma cells, macrophages), so in vitro cytotoxicity overstates clinical harm; the ZOE discrepancy shows this (Berman et al, 2021, via digest).
- No sealer is universally nontoxic; investigators report contradictory findings, and clinical and in vitro data often contradict each other (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010).

### Evidence by class
**ZOE.** Free eugenol contributes most to cytotoxicity of the fresh material, which falls as it sets (Gatewood, 2007). Marked in vitro, resorbs if extruded, long safe record (Berman et al, 2021, via digest).
**Epoxy resin.** Significant toxicity unset; at 24 h among the lowest of sealers (Gatewood, 2007). DNA damage was highest with epoxy sealers (Topseal, AH-26, AH Plus) in one study; AH-26 severely cytotoxic whereas Topseal and AH Plus were markedly lower (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010). AH-26 releases formaldehyde on setting (Berman et al, 2021, via digest).
**Polyketone.** Elevated cellular toxicity (Gatewood, 2007).
**Calcium hydroxide.** Least toxic of the eluates in one cell-culture study (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010).
**Glass ionomer.** Good biocompatibility (Gatewood, 2007).
**Methacrylate resin.** Elution compounds toxic to periodontal ligament cells and V79 cells in vitro; Epiphany more cytotoxic than Sealapex at 1 h but less at 24 h, less than Grossman's sealer; Epiphany significantly more toxic than RoekoSeal and EndoREZ with AH Plus intermediate (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010). In vivo primate: EndoREZ mean inflammation 1.5 at 26 days, 0.8 at 90 days; Epiphany 1.2 at 120 days; AH Plus 1.3-2 (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010). Guinea-pig study reported severe EndoREZ reaction and only Epiphany intraosseous biocompatibility: contradictory.
**Silicone.** RoekoSeal less toxic than Epiphany; GuttaFlow less toxic than AH Plus, Epiphany and Resilon at 72 h (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010).
**Bioceramic.** Considered more biocompatible and bioactive, but products vary in antimicrobial and cytotoxic effects (Guruswamy Pandian et al, 2026). MTA Fillapex showed genotoxicity unlike MTA, Biodentine and BC sealer (calcium_silicate Q2). Extrusion causes little periradicular inflammation compared with the severe reaction to gutta-percha extrusion (Berman et al, via calcium_silicate Q2). Even large extrusion into large lesions gave 96.8% healing in one series (calcium_silicate Q2).

### Monomer toxicology
TEGDMA and HEMA cause gene mutations, micronuclei and DNA strand breaks in vitro; monomers deplete glutathione and raise oxidative stress; conversion is at best below 70%, so unreacted monomer remains, and the oxygen-inhibited surface layer is highly toxic (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010). The same article states that neither EndoREZ nor Epiphany contains TEGDMA or HEMA; this contradicts the digest's statement that TEGDMA is present in "several" formulations (EndoREZ's own formulation is listed with triethylene glycol dimethacrylate in the same article), so the point is flagged.

### How to test and interpret
Cell-culture (in vitro) cytotoxicity, genotoxicity, subcutaneous or intraosseous implantation, and root-filling in animals (primates). The periapical tissue may react by inflammation, encapsulation, no reaction, or resorption of the sealer; biocompatibility is judged by resolution within 30-60 days (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010). Sample preparation (oxygen-inhibited layer) confounds results (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010).

## CRITICAL EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS
**Claim — "cytotoxicity" rankings of sealers are method-dependent and should not be treated as clinical rankings.** Direct contradictions exist for the same materials (EndoREZ mild irritant in primate and rat studies, severe in guinea pig; Epiphany more toxic or the only biocompatible depending on the study) (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010); unset toxicity is general and falls with setting (Berman et al, 2021, via digest; Gatewood, 2007). INTERPRETATION: avoid extrusion and prefer materials with favourable set toxicity and healing behaviour; use clinical outcome data rather than cell-culture rank. LIMITATION: few comparative clinical data; sample preparation artefacts. [Likely]

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 880; about 40 min)**

### Introduction
Cytotoxicity of a sealer matters because it contacts periradicular tissue through the apical foramen and lateral canals, and may be extruded. All sealers are toxic when fresh and much less toxic when set.

### Methods of evaluation
In vitro cell culture (periodontal ligament cells, fibroblasts), genotoxicity (DNA damage, micronuclei), implantation (subcutaneous, intraosseous), animal root-filling studies with histology.

### Cytotoxicity by sealer type
| Sealer | Findings |
|---|---|
| ZOE | Free eugenol cytotoxic when unset; marked in vitro; resorbs; safe long record |
| Epoxy (AH-26, AH Plus) | Toxic unset, lowest at 24 h; AH-26 formaldehyde; DNA damage in vitro |
| Polyketone (Diaket) | Elevated toxicity |
| Calcium hydroxide | Least toxic eluates in one study |
| Glass ionomer | Good |
| Methacrylate resin (EndoREZ, Epiphany) | Conflicting; mild initial irritation then healing in primates; unreacted monomer and oxygen-inhibited layer toxic |
| Silicone (RoekoSeal, GuttaFlow) | Lower than Epiphany and AH Plus |
| Bioceramic | Biocompatible, little inflammation when extruded; MTA Fillapex genotoxic |

### Factors modifying toxicity
Set versus unset; time (toxicity falls with 24 h to 72 h); extent of conversion; oxygen-inhibited layer; solubility and leaching; extrusion quantity; presence of infection; host defences.

### Interpretation
In vitro results exaggerate harm (no lymphatics or defence cells); contradictory results between studies; clinical success despite mild irritation; judge by healing within 30-60 days.

### Clinical implications
Avoid extrusion; adequate working length and control of seal; flush EDTA after NaOCl and a final saline or chlorhexidine rinse so that the oxygen-inhibited layer is minimised; prefer sealers with low unset toxicity in cases of likely extrusion (open apex).

### Conclusion
No sealer is entirely nontoxic; cytotoxicity declines with setting, and clinical success shows tolerance. Selection should rely on biological behaviour in vivo and extrusion control rather than on in vitro ranking.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Fresh toxic, set much less toxic.
- Cell cultures lack lymphatics and defence cells.
- ZOE: free eugenol; epoxy: lowest toxicity at 24 h; AH-26: formaldehyde.
- Resin: unreacted monomer, oxygen-inhibited layer.
- MTA Fillapex genotoxic; other MTA-based sealers acceptable.
