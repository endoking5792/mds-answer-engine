## STUDY MODE

### Definition
Non-eugenol sealers are root canal sealers that do not use eugenol as the liquid: calcium hydroxide-based (Sealapex, Apexit), polymer (epoxy AH-26/AH Plus, polyketone Diaket), glass ionomer (Ketac-Endo), methacrylate resin (EndoREZ, Epiphany), silicone (RoekoSeal), and calcium silicate sealers (Gatewood, 2007; Berman et al, 2021, via digest).

### Why avoid eugenol
Free eugenol is the main contributor to cytotoxicity of freshly mixed ZOE sealer (Gatewood, 2007). Eugenol-containing materials soften acrylic and interfere with the polymerisation of resins [Likely; eugenol-softens-acrylic is in the Sikri digest, the effect on adhesive resin cements is standard teaching not found in the sources read], which matters when resin-bonded restorations or resin sealers are planned. Eugenol allergy [RECALL].

### Merits (by example)
| Sealer | Merit |
|---|---|
| Calcium hydroxide (Sealapex) | Intended biological effect; adequate short-term seal; no eugenol cytotoxicity |
| Epoxy (AH Plus) | Good adhesion and handling; low toxicity at 24 h; low solubility |
| Glass ionomer | Chemical bond to dentin; good biocompatibility |
| Methacrylate resin | Hybrid layer; compatible with bonding; low viscosity |
| Bioceramic | Bioactive; no eugenol irritation; sustained alkalinity |

### Demerits
- Calcium hydroxide sealers: solubility needed for action opposes sealing; long-term stability questionable; biological effect not proven (Gatewood, 2007).
- Epoxy: toxic unset; AH-26 formaldehyde; no bond to gutta-percha.
- Glass ionomer: difficult to condition apically; hard and difficult to remove (Gatewood, 2007; Berman et al, 2021, via digest).
- Resin: moisture sensitivity; monomer toxicity; no root reinforcement (Pameijer and Zmener, 2010).
- Polyketone: handling and cellular toxicity (Gatewood, 2007).
- Loss of the antibacterial effect of eugenol and zinc (ZOE has inherent antibacterial properties) (Guruswamy Pandian et al, 2026).
- Several lack the long clinical record of ZOE (Gatewood, 2007).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 330; about 15 min)**

### Definition
Sealers that do not contain eugenol as a component: calcium hydroxide, epoxy resin, polyketone, glass ionomer, methacrylate resin, silicone and bioceramic sealers.

### Why needed
Free eugenol makes fresh ZOE sealer cytotoxic; eugenol can affect resin polymerisation and may soften acrylic.

### Merits
- No eugenol cytotoxicity or sensitivity.
- Better adhesion (epoxy, glass ionomer, resin).
- Low solubility (epoxy).
- Biological or bioactive potential (calcium hydroxide, bioceramic).
- Compatible with resin bonding.

### Demerits
- Calcium hydroxide: soluble, unproven effect.
- Epoxy: toxic when unset; formaldehyde (AH-26).
- Glass ionomer: difficult conditioning and retreatment.
- Resin: moisture and technique sensitivity; monomer toxicity.
- Lose ZOE's antibacterial action; shorter clinical record.

### Table
| Sealer | Merit | Demerit |
|---|---|---|
| Sealapex | Ca(OH)2 release | Solubility |
| AH Plus | Adhesion, low solubility | Unset toxicity |
| Ketac-Endo | Bond to dentin | Hard to remove |
| EndoREZ | Hybrid layer | Moisture sensitivity |

### Conclusion
Non-eugenol sealers avoid eugenol's irritation and interference but each trades something: solubility, toxicity, moisture sensitivity or retreatability.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Free eugenol = cytotoxicity of unset ZOE.
- Ca(OH)2 sealer solubility paradox.
- Glass ionomer sealer: bond but hard retreatment.
