# "Acid etching and bonding technique."

**Marks:** 10m, Short Note
**Sitting:** Apr 1997 (DM 90-00)

## Retrieval Report — AUDITED 2026-10-16

## STUDY MODE

**Step 1 — Isolation.** Rubber dam or adequate relative isolation to prevent saliva/moisture contamination throughout the procedure, since any contamination at any step reduces bond strength drastically. [RECALL] [Certain]

**Step 2 — Acid etching.** Apply phosphoric acid gel (30-40%) to enamel margins ± dentin (per chosen technique: total-etch = both simultaneously; selective-etch = enamel only); 15-20s on enamel, ~15s on dentin. Rinse thoroughly with water spray for an equal or longer duration.

**Step 3 — Drying/moisture control (the critical, technique-sensitive step).** Dry enamel to a **frosty-white** appearance (confirms adequate demineralization, safe to dry fully since enamel has no collapsible organic phase). Keep dentin **moist** — gently air-dry only to remove visible pooled water, leaving a glistening (not frosty, not wet-pooled) surface; do NOT desiccate, since the demineralized collagen meshwork collapses and loses porosity if over-dried, blocking subsequent resin infiltration.

**Step 4 — Primer/bonding-resin application, with real named-product examples (Summitt's Table 9-2, PDF p.441-448).** Scotchbond Multi-Purpose: primer dried 5s, bond cured 10s. Gluma Solid Bond: primer 30s, bond cure 40s. Gluma Comfort Bond: three consecutive coats, 15s each, cure 20s. Clearfil SE Bond (self-etch): primer 20s, two consecutive bond coats, dried from 2cm, cure 10s. [SRC]

**Step 5 — Solvent evaporation.** Gently air-thin the applied adhesive to evaporate the solvent (acetone/ethanol/water) fully — incomplete evaporation leaves voids/porosity in the cured adhesive layer, weakening the bond.

**Step 6 — Light-curing.** Light-cure the adhesive layer (typically 10-20s per manufacturer instructions) before applying/curing the overlying restorative material, locking the resin-infiltrated hybrid layer (dentin) and resin tags (enamel) in place.

**Step 7 — Restorative placement.** Place and cure the restorative material (composite resin, in most clinical contexts for this question), completing the bonded restoration.

## EXAM MODE
*(Estimated writing time: ~16 minutes)*

**Seven-step sequence:**
1. Isolation (rubber dam/relative isolation).
2. Acid etch (phosphoric acid, enamel ± dentin, 15-20s/15s).
3. Rinse → dry enamel to frosty-white; keep dentin MOIST (critical step).
4. Primer/bonding-resin application (wets/infiltrates the demineralized surface).
5. Solvent evaporation (air-thin) — incomplete evaporation weakens the bond.
6. Light-cure the adhesive layer.
7. Place/cure the restorative material.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Seven ordered steps: isolate → etch → rinse/differential-dry (enamel dry, dentin moist) → prime/bond → evaporate solvent → cure adhesive → place restorative.
- Moisture control at step 3 is the single most technique-sensitive, examinable point.
- Incomplete solvent evaporation (step 5) is a real, often-overlooked cause of bond weakness.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES
- Omitting isolation as the first step.
- Not distinguishing the enamel-dry vs dentin-moist drying requirement.
- Forgetting the solvent-evaporation step entirely.
