## STUDY MODE

**1. Universal/multi-mode adhesives (the dominant recent advance).** Single-bottle systems usable in etch-and-rinse, self-etch, or selective-etch mode, built around **10-MDP** functional-monomer chemistry providing genuine chemical (ionic MDP-calcium) adhesion alongside micromechanical hybrid-layer retention, with multi-substrate compatibility (zirconia, silica ceramics, metals). A 2022 systematic review/meta-analysis (pooling 15 studies) found no statistically significant dentin-bond-strength difference between etch-and-rinse and self-etch modes for this chemistry — strong, comparatively well-pooled evidence for this specific advance. **2. MMP-inhibition strategies.** Chlorhexidine pretreatment, EDTA conditioning, and newer targeted MMP-inhibitor monomers incorporated directly into experimental adhesive formulations, directly addressing the intrinsic, hydrolytic/enzymatic hybrid-layer degradation mechanism recognized as the field's central remaining durability problem. [RECALL] [Likely]

**3. Bioactive/remineralizing adhesives.** Bioactive-glass or calcium-phosphate-releasing fillers incorporated into experimental and some commercial adhesive systems, aiming to actively promote remineralization at the adhesive-dentin interface rather than relying solely on resin infiltration for durability.

**4. Antibacterial/bioactive monomers.** MDPB (12-methacryloyloxydodecylpyridinium bromide) — combining a bonding functional group with an antibacterial quaternary-ammonium moiety, targeting residual-bacteria-driven secondary caries risk at the bonded margin.

**5. Nanotechnology-enhanced adhesives.** Nano-hydroxyapatite/nano-silica fillers incorporated into adhesive resin formulations for improved mechanical properties and potentially reduced nanoleakage at the hybrid-layer/resin interface.

**6. Biomimetic remineralization approaches.** Ethanol wet-bonding/solvent-exchange (replacing water in demineralized collagen with ethanol for improved resin diffusion), and proanthocyanidin/phosphoprotein-analogue-based intrafibrillar collagen remineralization — a fundamentally different durability philosophy from resin infiltration alone.

**7. Refined selective-etch protocols**, continuing as the pragmatic contemporary clinical compromise between total-etch's enamel advantage and self-etch's dentin-gentleness/sensitivity advantage.

### Evidence-maturity note (a genuinely important critical point for a Recent-Advances framing specifically)
These seven advances are NOT equally evidence-backed: universal-adhesive performance has a real, pooled meta-analysis behind it; MMP-inhibitor, bioactive, nanotechnology, and biomimetic-remineralization strategies are predominantly supported by in-vitro/experimental data at this stage, with long-term clinical-trial-level outcome evidence still comparatively limited — this maturity gradient should be stated explicitly in a "recent advances" essay rather than treating every listed development as equally proven.
