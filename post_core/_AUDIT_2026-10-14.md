## A. Corrections to existing drafts (draft claim -> what sources say)
1. Q1 "6-million-patient US cohort, 97% at 8 y": not supported. Salehrabi & Rotstein = 1,462,936 teeth (BABA1; TORAB22 says 1.1 M patients); 97% retained at 8 y.
2. Q1 stiffness loss 5/40/60% (Sikri): Reeh = occlusal 20%, MO 46%, MOD 63%; endodontic treatment adds only 5% (BABA1; GUI8). Report both.
3. Q2/Q3/Q5 internal contradiction on fibre posts: Q2 says "ferrule-like effect without collar"; Q3/Q5 say ferrule mandatory. Sources: ferrule still needed (COH23 Fig 23.3; ING35; BABA4); dissent = Ferrari 2007 RCT (premolars, ferrule no benefit when almost no coronal structure; HARTY p.321) and Sikri p.392 ("not necessary, monoblock").
4. Core ferrule vs crown ferrule: 4 of 6 core-ferrule studies found no benefit; crown ferrule effective (ING35 pp.1094-5; TORAB p.359; BABA4 pp.149-150). Dissent: Weine p.563 prefers cast coping when <3 mm sound circumference.
5. "Ferrule 2 mm + biologic width 2 mm = 4 mm" (Q2/Q6): Gargiulo BW 2.04 mm (CT 1.07 + epith 0.97) EXCLUDES sulcus (0.69-0.75); Ingber/Nevins margin-to-crest >= 3 mm; Dibart 40% furcation lesions if <4 mm (mandibular molars, 5 y); 3.5-5.25 mm range; Sikri-E crest to remaining tooth 3.5-4.0 mm; Gutmann 2 mm tooth + 2.5 mm BW. Realistic supracrestal tooth need ~4.5-5 mm (ferrule 1.5-2 + BW 3). The "4 mm" is a minimum, not a settled figure.
6. Apical GP: 4-5 mm in drafts. Sources 3-7 mm; 5 mm recommended, 4 mm minimum (ING35; BABA4; TORAB; PROG10 4-6). Portell/Kvist: <3 mm leaks/more lesions; GUT20: no gap 83% vs gap >2 mm 29% success at 5 y.
7. Post width <= 1/3 root: PROG10 says <= 1/4 (0.5-1.5 mm); absolute maxima by tooth (Tilk: 0.6-0.7 mand incisors; 1.0 max central/palatal root; 0.8-0.9 others).
8. Threaded/active posts: drafts follow Marzouk. ING35: tapered threaded 20x more root fracture than parallel threaded; Creugers cast 91% vs threaded 81%; BABA1: 64 of 95 VRF teeth had threaded posts; COH/TORAB/P&P02/GUT: avoid. Retention: pre-tapped parallel threaded ~2x serrated, ~6x smooth tapered (Sikri-I). Weine p.554 dissent.
9. Pins: PG says should not be used in modern dentistry; Mount: contraindicated in most cases; TXT ch17 and MOD ch12 still teach them. (Pin topic stays excluded — separate "Pins" topic.)
10. Orthodontic extrusion vs crown lengthening: ADV ch10 prefers extrusion (CL worsens crown-root ratio, black triangles); COH23 limits extrusion posteriorly; Sikri-E35: extrusion no bone cutting/better esthetics but not for posteriors.
11. "Post does not strengthen" stated flatly: debated. Ferrari 2007 + Cagidiaco 2008 RCTs found higher survival with fibre posts (HARTY p.319); PG table says fibre posts "reinforce"; CG: bonded post "integral unit". Consensus (Baba, Tait, Dietschi, Ingle) = retention only.
12. GIC core ">=2 walls": Mount ch9 needs >= half original crown + 3 mm dentine collar, "misnomer"; PG: HVGIC only with 2 opposing walls; COH23: composite has replaced GIC; PROG10: GIC liner/base only.
13. Q1 anterior crown "no benefit": Salehrabi: 83% of extracted anteriors had no crown; Sorensen & Martinoff anterior 85.4% vs 87.5% (little difference). Present both.
14. Nagasiri "36% at 5 y without cuspal coverage" (Q1): CT p.141 gives 36% (Nagasiri); BABA gives 38% composite / 17% amalgam at 5 y (intact molars, access only) and 78% best-structure. Cite each with its own source/cohort.
15. Q5 "fibre post removal easier": Sikri-E22 p.396 easy; Sikri-E36 bonded/difficult; GUI/Baba: carbon easier than glass; zirconia irretrievable.
16. Q5 "carbon fibre radiolucent": radiopaque versions exist (BABA3 p.95; Composipost, Snowpost).
17. Dentin modulus: Craig/Michigan 19 GPa; Phillips 11.7 GPa; Cohen 16.5-18.5; PROG10 12-18. Fibre post modulus: "similar to dentin" (ADH ~15 GPa) vs "3-4x dentine" (HARTY p.321) vs 10-25 GPa (GUI8). State range.
18. Amalgam core timing: 24 h (Tait) vs >=70% strength at 8 h (Phillips) / fast-set same-visit (CG); BABA: "cannot be prepared right away".
19. RMGIC for posts: do not use (hygroscopic expansion, root fracture concern; COH23; BABA3); Phillips notes 12-month in vitro no cracking under all-ceramic.
20. Coronal seal timing: 19 days (Torabinejad), Cavit/IRM/TempBond leaked at 30 d, restored within 2 wk higher survival (775 teeth); crowned <4 months 85% vs 68% (CC ch8); drafts' "2 weeks" is a convention, not a threshold.

## B. Gaps filled
- Q6 surgical crown lengthening (draft's disclosed FAILURE): BABA7 (technique, healing 5 months stable, impressions 6-8 wk non-esthetic / 8-12 wk esthetic, contraindications), ADV ch4 (apically repositioned flap, 3-6 mo), BABA8 forced eruption (35-60 g, fiberotomy to ~3 mm apical to crest, 4-10 wk stabilisation, >=4 mm tooth above crest), Goodacre via TORAB p.351 (rapid vs slow extrusion), TRI scoring (Sikri-E22 p.374-5; McDonald & Setchell), Dental Practicality Index (named only).
- Q7 endocrowns (draft's "no outcome data"): definition/indications Sikri-E22 p.403; prep ADH p.197 (>=2 mm occlusal reduction, no undercuts, orifice subseal, supragingival margin); survival: Bindl 145 molars (70 endocrowns) ~5 y classic 95% / reduced 92% / endo 87% (ns); Fages 7 y >98% both; Otto & Mormann 12 y molars 90.5% vs 95% (ns); Belleflamme 99 endocrowns 4 +/- 3 y 99% survival 90% success (GUI8 s.8.6.3). Baba has NO endocrown content. ESE 2021 accepts endocrowns.
- Q3 methods of stress analysis ([RECALL] in draft): brittle coating, strain gauges, holography, 2-D/3-D photoelasticity, FEA (Michigan Ch.4); ESPI (COH23); Henry/Standlee photoelastic, Ross strain gauge, 2-D FEA (post reduced dentine stress up to 20% axial, 3-8% at 45 deg; ING35 pp.1085-7).

## C. High-value new facts (by question)
Q1: Ng meta-analysis survival 93% (2 y) / 92% (5 y) / 88% (10 y); cast/crown vs plastic OR 3.5; both proximal contacts OR 2.6; coronal leakage OR 10.7 (COH17). Gillen: good RCT+good restoration OR ~2.7-2.8. Sorensen & Martinoff table; failure w/ vs w/o crown max premolar 6% vs 44%, max molar 2% vs 50%, mand premolar 6% vs 38%, mand molar 3% vs 42% (BABA1). Hansen 20 y max premolar MOD amalgam w/o cuspal coverage fractured 28/57/73% at 3/10/20 y. Cochrane (Fedorowicz): no evidence for crown over filling; Mannocci RCT post+composite = crown at 3 y premolars; Ng: "systematic full crown an exaggeration". Post-and-core failure mean 9% (6-14%) over 8 y; loosening 56% of failures; root fracture tapered cemented 2%, threaded 7%, parallel 1% (ING35 Tables 35-1..4). Post space perforation 0-7% (0.7%, 1.1%, 2.3%, 5.4% referral). ETT extraction reasons: molars 52% nonrestorable fracture vs 11.5% RCT failure (GUI8). Biting force: anterior 25-75 N, posterior 40-125 N, parafunction >=1000 N (COH23).
Q2: Libman & Nicholls 1.5-2 mm effective, 0.5-1.0 ineffective; Pereira 3 > 2 > 1 mm; Tan: uniform 2 mm 360 deg > non-uniform; ferrule cut vertical root fracture by 1/3, failures mostly horizontal (retrievable); 3-4 coronal walls better survival (Ferrari 2011, 240 fibre posts); Skupien meta: P = 0.002 overall, 0.05 premolars, 0.559 molars, 0.329 anteriors; Santana molar fracture loads 2000-2900 N with ferrule vs 1500-2100 N without (>> physiologic).
Q3: post length rules (8 published; only 34% of 327 posts as long as crown; 5% 2/3-3/4 root; loosened posts 59% of ideal length; 3/4 root up to 30% more retentive); length +3 mm = 40% more retention (Sikri); molar posts <= 7 mm below orifice (Abou-Rass, 150 molars; 20/75 mesial roots perforated/thinned); No.4 Gates-Glidden 7.3% furcal perforation, Peeso No.3 = GG No.4 = ISO 110; distal root mand molar furcal wall <1.0 mm in 82%; parallel posts more apical stress (cementation) vs tapered more cervical/wedging in function; deflection premolar 1.0 -> 16/20/24/28 um (Class I/MO/MOD/pulpotomy).
Q4/Q5: SIKRI-E22 wall-based protocol (1 wall missing fill, 2 core+crown, 3 post+coverage, 4 post +/- CL/OE); Grossman flowchart (stop criteria); fibre post failure ranges (CFR 0-35% over 2.7-6.7 y; GFR 0-11.4%; zirconia 0-9%); fibre post failure mode debonding vs metal root fracture; Weine/Cochrane Bolla 2007 only one RCT; zinc phosphate vs resin cement; self-etch adhesives not indicated with some dual-cure cements (Craig); Ribbond protocol.
