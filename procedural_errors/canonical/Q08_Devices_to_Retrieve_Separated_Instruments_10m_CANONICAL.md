## Explanation

Cohen's Ch.20 groups removal methods as mechanical (extractors, wire loops, post removal systems, ultrasonics, laser), chemical (solvents, electrochemical dissolution) and surgical; fragments above the orifice are grasped with a haemostat, Steiglitz forceps or a modified Castroviejo needle holder; a bypassed fragment can be pulled out by braiding Hedström files around it; extractors need about 2 mm of the fragment exposed; the Masserann kit cannot be used beyond the midroot or in curved canals because it sacrifices dentin; the Endo Safety System uses smaller trephines; a tube extractor can be bonded to the fragment with cyanoacrylate; the Canal Finder and EndoPuls systems vibrate a file 1-2 mm vertically (Terauchi and Renton, 2021) [SRC]. Ingle's describes the modified Gates-Glidden drill cut at its widest diameter for a staging platform, ultrasonic tips moved counterclockwise, a hypodermic needle to expose the head, the File Removal System (microtubes with a side window and 45-degree bevel, plus screw wedges; black tube 1.0 mm), and the Endo Rescue kit (cylindrical bur, short GG-3 and GG-4, centering drill and trephine used counterclockwise) (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019) [SRC]. Torabinejad's 6th edition: microtrephine at 600 rpm counterclockwise; Terauchi File Retrieval Kit loop needing a space 0.4 mm wide and 0.5 mm long (Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021) [SRC]. The operating microscope underlies all: "If you can see it, you can probably do it" (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019) [SRC].

## ✎ Exam core

**Introduction.** Separated instruments are removed by mechanical, chemical or surgical means; mechanical devices are the mainstay, used under the operating microscope (Terauchi and Renton, 2021; Machtou and Ruddle, 2019).

**1. Visualisation:** dental operating microscope, fibre-optic light; CBCT for planning.

**2. Access and exposure instruments**
- Modified Gates-Glidden drill (cut across at its widest diameter) to make a staging platform (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019).
- Microtrephines (rotated counterclockwise, about 600 rpm) (Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021).
- Hypodermic needle used by hand to expose the head (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019).

**3. Ultrasonic instruments:** piezoelectric unit with thin, long tips (diamond-coated or titanium, e.g. ProUltra, TFRK-S), used dry, counterclockwise, at low power. NiTi fragments may fracture again (Terauchi and Renton, 2021).

**4. Grasping instruments (fragment in the chamber):** Steiglitz forceps, haemostat, modified Castroviejo needle holder, Perry pliers (Terauchi and Renton, 2021).

**5. Tube-and-extractor systems (need about 2 mm exposed)**
| Device | Principle |
|---|---|
| Masserann kit | Trephines + extractor tube with stylet; straight canals only, removes much dentin (Terauchi and Renton, 2021) |
| Endo Safety System | Smaller trephines and own extractor |
| Instrument Removal System (IRS) | Microtube + wedge (Hülsmann and Chong, 2017) |
| File Removal System (FRS) | Microtubes with side window and 45° bevel, screw wedges (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019) |
| Endo Rescue kit | Cylindrical bur, short GG-3/GG-4, centering drill, trephine used counterclockwise |
| Tube bonded with cyanoacrylate or composite | Tube glued over fragment, then rotated counterclockwise |
| Terauchi File Retrieval Kit (loop) | Wire loop slipped over the fragment; needs a space 0.4 × 0.5 mm (Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021) |

**6. File-based methods:** braiding several Hedström files around a bypassed fragment; Canal Finder/EndoPuls (vertical vibration 1-2 mm) (Terauchi and Renton, 2021).

**7. Others:** laser; chemical and electrochemical dissolution (experimental).

**Choice of device:** fragments in the straight part of the canal suit ultrasonics and microtubes; fragments around a curve need thin ultrasonic tips on the inner wall or a loop; Masserann-type trephines are avoided in thin or curved roots because they remove dentin and risk perforation (Terauchi and Renton, 2021; Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021).

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Microscope + ultrasonics + microtubes = most predictable.
- Masserann: straight canals only (dentin loss).
- Loop (TFRK) needs 0.4 × 0.5 mm space.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES
- Listing devices without stating that most need 2 mm (one-third) of the fragment exposed.
- Recommending Masserann in curved canals.

## MNEMONIC
**"See, Shake, Snare"**: microscope, ultrasonics, tube or loop.
