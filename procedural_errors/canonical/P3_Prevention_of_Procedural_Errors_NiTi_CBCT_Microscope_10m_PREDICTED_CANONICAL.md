## Explanation

Torabinejad's 6th edition: CBCT "helps the clinician to prevent most of the procedural accidents"; axial slices show canal number and wall thickness, sagittal and coronal slices show curvature (Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021) [SRC]. Cohen's Ch.20: CBCT shows the ledge and the original canal better than 2D; reciprocation lowers errors even for novices; heat-treated NiTi centres better; non-cutting tips slip along the wall; GentleWave cleans beyond obstructions (Terauchi and Renton, 2021) [SRC]. Grossman's: NiTi in the right protocol is the safest way to shape complex canals (Gopikrishna, 2021) [SRC]. Ingle's: the microscope is now the standard of vision (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019) [SRC].

## ✎ Exam core

**1. CBCT (Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021; Terauchi and Renton, 2021):** axial slices show number of canals and wall thickness (prevents perforation); sagittal and coronal slices show curvature and its direction (prevents ledges and separation); locates ledges, fragments and perforations for planning.

**2. Magnification (operating microscope):** finds calcified and extra canals, shows fragments and ledges directly; "if you can see it, you can probably do it" (Machtou and Ruddle, 2019).

**3. Electronic apex locators:** accurate working length (prevents over- and under-instrumentation) and detection of perforations (Gopikrishna, 2021).

**4. NiTi instruments:** flexible, centred preparation with less transportation (Gopikrishna, 2021); non-cutting tips slip along the wall; heat-treated alloys (controlled memory, Gold) centre better; reciprocation reduces ledges and separation even for novices and extends file life (Terauchi and Renton, 2021; Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021).

**5. Glide path instruments** (hand or rotary pathfinding files) before shaping (Hülsmann and Chong, 2017).

**6. Torque-controlled motors** with built-in apex locators [RECALL for torque control; built-in apex locator from Nosrat, Terauchi and Torabinejad, 2021].

**7. Irrigation systems:** GentleWave can disinfect beyond ledges without shaping (Terauchi and Renton, 2021); side-vented needles reduce extrusion.

**8. Bioceramics (MTA, Biodentine)** for immediate perforation repair, which limits the damage when an error does occur.

**Which advance prevents which error**
| Error | Advance |
|---|---|
| Access perforation, missed canals | CBCT axial slices, microscope |
| Ledge, transportation, zip | CBCT curvature views, flexible heat-treated NiTi, non-cutting tips, glide path |
| Instrument separation | Reciprocation, single use, glide path, wet canal |
| Over-instrumentation, apical perforation | Apex locator, motor with built-in apex locator |
| Irrigant extrusion | Side-vented needles; negative-pressure or multisonic systems [RECALL] |

**Training:** simulation and practice on extracted teeth and plastic blocks, and case-difficulty assessment before treatment, remain the first line of prevention [RECALL].

**Limitation:** technology reduces but does not remove errors; NiTi separation is still more frequent than hand-file separation, and technique, glide path and training remain essential.
