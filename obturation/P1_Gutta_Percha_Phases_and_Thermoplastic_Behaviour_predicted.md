## STUDY MODE

### Phases
Gutta-percha, like water, has phase changes with temperature (Weine). As supplied it is **beta**: solid, compactable, elongatable, brittle when aged and not adhesive. On heating it becomes **alpha**: runny, tacky, sticky, non-compactable (Weine). A third phase (gamma/amorphous) occurs at higher temperature with poorly known properties (Weine; Torabinejad et al).

| Source | Beta to alpha | Higher phase |
|---|---|---|
| Weine | 42-49 °C | Gamma at 56-62 °C |
| Sikri | 42-49 °C | not stated |
| Torabinejad et al | 42-49 °C | Amorphous above 53-59 °C |
| Azim and Aksel | Heated to 70 °C gives alpha | not stated |
| Cohen's; Grossman's | Alpha melts above 65 °C | not stated |

### Volumetric behaviour
Materials expand when heated and shrink when cooled; once placed in the canal and heated, gutta-percha expands, and shrinks during cooling, so every heating technique must include a compaction phase to compensate (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019; Weine). Gutta-percha is neither molecularly condensable nor compressible; "compaction, not compression" occurs, by collapse of internal voids (Schilder et al, in Rotstein and Ingle, 2019). It shrinks when chemically softened too, as the solvent evaporates (24% volume loss in the old Callahan-Johnston method; Sikri, 2019).

### Alpha versus beta in practice
Alpha-phase gutta-percha is used in thermoplastic techniques, beta-phase in lateral condensation (Gopikrishna, 2021). Alpha gutta-percha that has been heated and cooled shows less shrinkage and is more dimensionally stable for thermoplastic techniques; slow cooling recrystallises alpha, routine cooling gives beta (Berman et al, 2021). Gopikrishna says the thermoplasticised alpha phase shrinks as it sets. The books disagree on emphasis; the practical consequence is the same: keep apical pressure while it cools (System B holds pressure 10 s; continuous wave 5-10 s).

### Related behaviours
- **Poor heat conductor:** controls plasticity at the apex when heated (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019).
- **Thermafil gutta-percha** behaves like alpha, resolidifies in about 90 s, and is not changed by reheating (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019).
- **Low-fusing** alpha gutta-percha (MicroSeal, Ultrafil) gives lower fusion point, longer working time and less cooling shrinkage (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019).
- **Heat to the tooth:** root surface rise believed harmful above 10 °C; System B and Touch 'n Heat stayed under 10 °C in maxillary teeth; flame carriers exceeded it (Berman et al, 2021).

### Introduction
Gutta-percha is a trans-polyisoprene whose crystalline form changes with temperature. This explains why warm obturation techniques work and why they need compaction.

### Phases
- **Beta (room temperature):** solid, compactable, brittle with age, non-adhesive; the form of commercial cones.
- **Alpha (heated, about 42-49 °C onward; some books say above 65-70 °C):** tacky, flowable, non-compactable.
- **Gamma/amorphous (about 53-62 °C and above):** properties not well known.
Books differ on the temperatures, so state a range and the source.

### Volume changes
Gutta-percha expands on heating and shrinks as it cools. A heated mass must therefore be compacted while it cools: a heat plugger in System B is held under pressure for about 10 s, injected material is compacted with pluggers, and carriers are held until set. Solvent softening shrinks by evaporation and was abandoned.

### Clinical consequences
1. **Alpha-phase** cones and pellets are used in warm vertical, injectable and carrier techniques; **beta-phase** cones in lateral compaction.
2. **Low-fusing** alpha gutta-percha lowers working temperature and cooling shrinkage.
3. **Poor thermal conduction** keeps heat from travelling far apically.
4. **Root surface temperature** should not rise more than about 10 °C; keep heated pluggers 4-5 mm from the apex and avoid flame-heated carriers.
5. **A sealer is always needed** to fill the interface left by shrinkage.

### Conclusion
The beta-alpha transition lets gutta-percha adapt to canal anatomy, but cooling shrinkage makes compaction under pressure and a sealer essential.
