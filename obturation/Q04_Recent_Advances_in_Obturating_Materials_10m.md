## STUDY MODE

### Why new materials
Gutta-percha does not bond to dentin and shrinks on cooling; sealers are soluble or non-bonding. New materials try to bond core, sealer and dentin (monoblock), flow without heat, or add bioactivity (Berman et al, 2021; Sikri, 2019).

### Advances
1. **Resin core systems.** Resilon with Epiphany/RealSeal (polycaprolactone core, dual-cure resin sealer, primer). Withdrawn in 2014 after Barborka reported 56% success at 6 years against 88% for gutta-percha (Azim and Aksel, 2022). RealSeal One: polysulfone carrier coated with Resilon (Sikri, 2019).
2. **Coated cones.** Resin-coated (EndoREZ points); glass-ionomer-coated (Activ GP, Activ GP Precision); bioceramic-nanoparticle-coated cones used with bioceramic sealers (Azim and Aksel, 2022). Bacterial leakage of Activ GP/GIC sealer, Resilon/Epiphany and gutta-percha/AH Plus showed no significant difference at 65 days (Berman et al, 2021).
3. **Carrier-based cross-linked gutta-percha.** GuttaCore: core of cross-linked gutta-percha, surface alpha gutta-percha, no plastic carrier, removable; limited studies (Sikri, 2019; Azim and Aksel, 2022).
4. **Cold flowable gutta-percha.** GuttaFlow, GuttaFlow 2 (microsilver, 0.2% expansion, working time 10-15 min), GuttaFlow Bioseal (calcium silicate) (Sikri, 2019; Berman et al, 2021).
5. **Hydrophilic expanding points.** SmartSeal: Propoint (nylon-polymer core, swelling coating) with Smartpaste or Smartpaste Bio; the point expands laterally within 4 h; "limited documentation" (Sikri, 2019).
6. **Bioceramic obturation.** Hydraulic calcium silicate sealers with single cone or coneless techniques, BC HiFlow for warm techniques, BC putty for apical plugs and open apices; synchronised hydraulic condensation with proprietary coated cones (Azim and Aksel, 2022; Common Complications). Retrievability debated (Jain, 2016 vs Berman et al, 2021).
7. **Bonded fibre systems.** FiberFill: glass-fibre post with gutta-percha tip and dual-cure sealer (Sikri, 2019). EZ Fill: bi-directional spiral with epoxy sealer, room temperature (Sikri, 2019).
8. **Future.** Nanodiamond-embedded gutta-percha in clinical trials with no adverse events reported (Berman et al, 2021).
9. **Not recommended.** Medicated gutta-percha (calcium hydroxide, iodoform, chlorhexidine): effectiveness not shown (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019; Berman et al, 2021).

### Evidence
Cohen's: little evidence supports one method of obturation over another on outcome (Berman et al, 2021). Azim and Aksel: no significant outcome difference between gutta-percha-and-sealer techniques. New materials are promising in vitro but lack long-term clinical data; the one resin core studied long term did worse.

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 384; about 17 min at 22 words/min)**

### Introduction
Gutta-percha lacks adhesion and shrinks on cooling, and most sealers are soluble or non-bonding. Recent obturating materials try to bond core, sealer and dentin (the monoblock concept), to flow without heat, to expand to fill the canal, or to add bioactivity. Their long-term clinical evidence is limited.

### Recent advances
**1. Resin core systems.** Resilon (polycaprolactone with bioactive glass) with Epiphany dual-cure sealer and self-etch primer aimed at a monoblock. Barborka found 56% success at 6 years against 88% for gutta-percha, and a later study found 5.3 times more periapical lesions; Resilon was withdrawn in 2014. RealSeal One used a polysulfone carrier coated with Resilon.

**2. Coated cones.** Gutta-percha coated with resin (EndoREZ points), glass ionomer (Activ GP, Activ GP Precision) or bioceramic nanoparticles, used with matching sealers to form a bonded unit. A bacterial leakage study found no significant difference between Activ GP, Resilon and gutta-percha with AH Plus at 65 days.

**3. GuttaCore.** A carrier-based obturator whose carrier is cross-linked gutta-percha bonded to alpha-phase gutta-percha. No plastic carrier gap; easily removed for post space and retreatment; limited studies.

**4. Cold flowable gutta-percha.** GuttaFlow, GuttaFlow 2 (microsilver, expansion about 0.2%, working time 10-15 min) and GuttaFlow Bioseal (calcium silicate) combine gutta-percha powder under 30 μm with a silicone matrix.

**5. Hydrophilic expanding points.** SmartSeal: a Propoint with a nylon core and swelling coating plus Smartpaste or Smartpaste Bio; expansion occurs in the first four hours; documentation is limited.

**6. Bioceramic obturation.** Calcium silicate sealers with single-cone, coneless and BioConeless techniques; synchronised hydraulic condensation with coated cones; heat-tolerant BC HiFlow for warm techniques; bioceramic putty for apical plugs. Retreatability is disputed.

**7. Bonded fibre and spiral systems.** FiberFill (glass-fibre post with gutta-percha tip and dual-cure sealer); EZ Fill (bi-directional spiral with epoxy sealer, room temperature).

**8. Future.** Nanodiamond-embedded gutta-percha, in clinical trials with no adverse events reported.

**9. Not recommended.** Medicated gutta-percha containing calcium hydroxide, iodoform or chlorhexidine: effectiveness not demonstrated.

### Evidence and limitations
Little evidence supports one obturation method or material over another on outcome. New materials show promise in vitro, but the one bonded system followed long term, Resilon, did worse. Bioceramic sealers lack sufficient human clinical comparison.

### Conclusion
Newer materials improve handling, flow and biology on the bench, but none has yet outperformed gutta-percha with a conventional sealer in clinical outcome.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Resilon withdrawn 2014 (Barborka 56% vs 88%).
- Coated cones: resin, glass ionomer, bioceramic.
- GuttaCore cross-linked; GuttaFlow cold flowable.
- No outcome superiority proven.
