## STUDY MODE
**Principle.** Friction between a rotating instrument and gutta-percha generates heat that softens the gutta-percha and drives it apically and laterally (Sikri, 2019; Berman et al, 2021).

**McSpadden compactor (1979).** A reverse Hedström file in a latch-type handpiece, rotated at 8,000-20,000 rpm alongside a sealer-coated master cone fitted short of length. Friction-softened gutta-percha is forced apically and laterally as the compactor is withdrawn (Sikri, 2019; Rotstein and Ingle, 2019). Rotary compactors in nickel-titanium followed.

**Modified McSpadden (MicroSeal).** NiTi condensers at 1,000-4,000 rpm with pre-plasticised alpha-phase (low-fusing) gutta-percha from a MicroSeal heater; flows laterally by centrifugal force; lower fusion point, longer working time, less shrinkage; needs good apical control (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019; Sikri, 2019).

**Other compactors.** Maillefer Guttacondenser, Zipperer engine plugger, Quickfill (titanium core coated with alpha-phase gutta-percha; core may be left in place) (Sikri, 2019).

**Advantages.** Simple armamentarium, fills irregularities, fast (Berman et al, 2021).

**Disadvantages.** Extrusion; compactor fracture; gouging of walls; cannot be used in curved canals; frictional heat, which can cause resorption and ankylosis (Berman et al, 2021; Sikri, 2019).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 77; about 4 min at 22 words/min)**

### Principle
Gutta-percha is softened by frictional heat from a rotating compactor and driven apically and laterally.

### Instruments
McSpadden compactor (reverse Hedström, 8,000-20,000 rpm); NiTi MicroSeal condensers (1,000-4,000 rpm); Guttacondenser, Zipperer, Quickfill.

### Technique
Fit the master cone short of length, apply sealer, insert a compactor alongside it to 3-4 mm from the prepared length, activate, and withdraw as the mass is compacted.

### Advantages and disadvantages
Quick, fills irregularities; but instrument fracture, extrusion, heat, unsuitable for curved canals.
