## STUDY MODE
**Principle.** Friction between a rotating instrument and gutta-percha generates heat that softens the gutta-percha and drives it apically and laterally (Sikri, 2019; Berman et al, 2021).

**McSpadden compactor (1979).** A reverse Hedström file in a latch-type handpiece, rotated at 8,000-20,000 rpm alongside a sealer-coated master cone fitted short of length. Friction-softened gutta-percha is forced apically and laterally as the compactor is withdrawn (Sikri, 2019; Rotstein and Ingle, 2019). Rotary compactors in nickel-titanium followed.

**Modified McSpadden (MicroSeal).** NiTi condensers at 1,000-4,000 rpm with pre-plasticised alpha-phase (low-fusing) gutta-percha from a MicroSeal heater; flows laterally by centrifugal force; lower fusion point, longer working time, less shrinkage; needs good apical control (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019; Sikri, 2019).

**Other compactors.** Maillefer Guttacondenser, Zipperer engine plugger, Quickfill (titanium core coated with alpha-phase gutta-percha; core may be left in place) (Sikri, 2019).

**Advantages.** Simple armamentarium, fills irregularities, fast (Berman et al, 2021).

**Disadvantages.** Extrusion; compactor fracture; gouging of walls; cannot be used in curved canals; frictional heat, which can cause resorption and ankylosis (Berman et al, 2021; Sikri, 2019).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 193; about 9 min at 22 words/min)**

### Principle
Frictional heat generated between a rotating instrument and gutta-percha softens the gutta-percha, and the instrument drives the softened mass apically and laterally against the canal walls.

### Instruments and technique
- **McSpadden compactor (1979):** a reverse Hedström file in a latch-type handpiece at 8,000-20,000 rpm. A master cone is fitted short of the prepared length and sealer applied; the compactor is inserted alongside to 3-4 mm from the prepared length, activated, and withdrawn as the pliable mass is compacted apically and laterally.
- **Modified McSpadden (MicroSeal):** NiTi condensers at 1,000-4,000 rpm coated with pre-plasticised low-fusing alpha-phase gutta-percha from a heater; the mass flows laterally by centrifugal force. Lower fusion point, longer working time and less cooling shrinkage, but it needs good apical control.
- **Others:** Guttacondenser, Zipperer engine plugger (for back-fill) and Quickfill (titanium core coated with alpha-phase gutta-percha).

### Advantages
Simple armamentarium; fills canal irregularities; quick.

### Disadvantages
Instrument fracture; extrusion of material; gouging of canal walls; cannot be used in curved canals; excessive heat can cause resorption and ankylosis;.

### Conclusion
Thermomechanical compaction is a rapid warm technique, but its fracture and overheating risks have reduced its use in favour of device-controlled warm techniques.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- McSpadden 1979; reverse Hedström; 8,000-20,000 rpm.
- MicroSeal NiTi condensers 1,000-4,000 rpm, alpha-phase.
- Not for curved canals.
