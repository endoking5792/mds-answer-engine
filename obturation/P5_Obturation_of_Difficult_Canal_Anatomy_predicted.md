## STUDY MODE

| Anatomy | Problem | Suitable obturation |
|---|---|---|
| **Curved canals** | Rigid spreaders and pluggers cannot reach the apex; more vertical force with straight instruments | Lateral compaction with **flexible spreaders**, master cone 2 mm short of the terminus; in severe curvature or dilaceration lateral compaction is not feasible and thermoplasticised gutta-percha is preferred (Sikri, 2019). Flexible plastic carriers (Thermafil); NiTi MicroSeal condensers (Sikri, 2019). |
| **Oval canals** | A round cone fits only in two points; single cone leaves sealer-filled gaps | Warm vertical compaction, injectable back-fill, or carrier-based; ZAP/TAP method with Endotec in C-shaped canals (Sikri, 2019); single cone is weak when the cone does not fit (Chopra, Ch.12). |
| **C-shaped canals** | Isthmuses and fins | Obtura-type injection indicated; warm lateral (Endotec ZAP/TAP) (Sikri, 2019). |
| **Internal resorption, webbings, lateral/accessory canals** | Irregular spaces | Injectable thermoplasticised gutta-percha; Thermafil also fills internal resorptive defects, fins and lateral canals (Sikri, 2019; Berman et al, 2021). |
| **Apical deltas, abrupt apical curvature** | High pressure needed to push material into the cleaned area | BioConeless technique with warm gutta-percha (Chopra, Ch.12). |
| **Open apex, apical resorption** | No apical stop | MTA plug, custom cone, dentin chips (Q10). |
| **Teeth needing a post** | Post space needs gutta-percha removal | Apical sectional plug (SimpliFill, warm segment) (Sikri, 2019); avoid carriers that are hard to remove. |

Common cautions: extrusion with thermoplastic techniques; heat damage; vertical root fracture from excessive force (Sikri, 2019).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 141; about 6 min at 22 words/min)**

### Introduction
Cold lateral compaction fills regular canals well but not irregular ones. Complex anatomy needs techniques that flow or adapt.

### Curved canals
Flexible spreaders; master cone 2 mm short; thermoplasticised gutta-percha or a flexible carrier for severe curvature. MicroSeal NiTi condensers.

### Oval and C-shaped canals
A round single cone cannot fill these; use warm vertical compaction, injectable gutta-percha or carriers. Endotec with the ZAP and TAP method helps C-shaped molars.

### Internal resorption, webbings, accessory canals
Injectable thermoplasticised gutta-percha (Obtura) or Thermafil.

### Apical deltas and abrupt curvature
Warm gutta-percha with a bioceramic sealer (coneless technique).

### Open apex
MTA apical plug, custom cone or dentin chips.

### Precautions
Control the apical limit to avoid extrusion; limit heat; avoid excessive compaction force.

### Conclusion
Match the technique to the anatomy: warm and flowable materials for complex canals, flexible instruments for curves, barriers for open apices.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Severe curvature: thermoplasticised gutta-percha.
- C-shaped: Obtura, Endotec ZAP/TAP.
- Single cone poor in oval canals.
