## STUDY MODE
Open apices (immature roots, apical resorption, iatrogenic enlargement) lack an apical stop, so a conventional master cone does not fit and material extrudes. Options:

1. **Apical barrier before obturation.** MTA placed 3-6 mm apically (Azim and Aksel, 2022) or 4-5 mm (Sikri, 2019) with a plugger; a resorbable collagen matrix (CollaCote, CollaPlug) can prevent extrusion; moist cotton pellet against the MTA; check set at next visit; back-fill with injectable gutta-percha. MTA gives a biocompatible, hydrophilic seal and is "the technique of choice" (Azim and Aksel, 2022); high success in apexification. Beware bismuth-oxide discolouration near the crown; BC putty is easier to place (Azim and Aksel, 2022). Do not etch over MTA (Azim and Aksel, 2022).
2. **Dentin chips** pushed apically with a paper point, 1-2 mm, as a biologic plug; prevents overfilling; infected chips may impair repair (Sikri, 2019).
3. **Custom cone / solvent technique.** The master cone is softened in chloroform and tamped into the canal for an impression of the apical part, or several cones are heated and rolled together; reduces extrusion and may improve the seal (Berman et al, 2021; Azim and Aksel, 2022). Solvent shrinkage is the drawback.
4. **Modified lateral compaction.** Inverted cone (serrated butt end cut and the coarse cone inverted) or roll-cone; cold lateral compaction follows (Sikri, 2019).
5. **Carrier-based and warm gutta-percha.** Thermafil useful in open apices (Sikri, 2019); sectional/warm apical gutta-percha with sealer.
6. **Apical plug systems:** SimpliFill (5 mm apical gutta-percha plug on a carrier); FiberFill (Sikri, 2019).

For a vital pulp, instrumentation and filling need not reach the terminus: an apical dentin-chip block can promote repair and apical closure (Azim and Aksel, 2022).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 87; about 4 min at 22 words/min)**

An open apex has no apical stop, so a conventional cone overextends. Techniques:
- **MTA apical plug** (3-6 mm) with collagen matrix, then gutta-percha back-fill. First choice.
- **Dentin chip plug** (1-2 mm), a biologic barrier.
- **Custom or inverted cone**, softened in chloroform, to mould to the apex.
- **Roll-cone technique:** heated cones rolled together, chilled, fitted, then lateral compaction.
- **Thermafil/warm gutta-percha** with sealer.
- Bioceramic putty as an alternative to MTA.

Precautions: avoid overfilling, avoid etching over MTA, discolouration from bismuth oxide.
