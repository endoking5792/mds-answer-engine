## STUDY MODE

### Why it is a problem
Gutta-percha is thermolabile and cannot be heat sterilised (Sikri, 2019; Gopikrishna, 2021). Cones are sterilised by the manufacturer, but contamination was found in 5-8% of cones taken straight from the package and in almost 20% after opening, from the clinical environment and handling (Azim and Aksel, 2022). Gutta-percha has a smooth surface and contains zinc oxide, which keep contamination low (Azim and Aksel, 2022).

### Methods
| Agent | Result in the textbooks |
|---|---|
| **5.25% NaOCl, 1 min** | Standard method; also killed *Bacillus subtilis* spores in the study cited by Cohen's (Berman et al, 2021; Sikri, 2019; Gopikrishna, 2021). Ingle's: 5-6% NaOCl for 60 s eliminates Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria and even *B. subtilis* spores (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019). Lower concentrations have also been tried but higher is preferred (Sikri, 2019). |
| **Rinse after NaOCl** | Hydrogen peroxide or ethyl alcohol (Sikri, 2019) to remove NaOCl crystals. |
| **2% chlorhexidine** | **Conflict:** effective within 5 min (Sikri, 2019) vs not effective against *B. subtilis* spores (Berman et al, 2021). |
| **2% glutaraldehyde, 70% ethyl alcohol** | Not effective against *B. subtilis* spores (Berman et al, 2021). |
| **10% polyvinylpyrrolidone-iodine** | Effective in 30 s to 1 min (Sikri, 2019). |
| **Paraformaldehyde vapour** | Effective in 1 h (Sikri, 2019). |

### Practical points
- Immerse for 1 min, then rinse; the procedure should not alter the structure of the cone (Azim and Aksel, 2022).
- Notch the master cone at the reference point and dip it in 5% NaOCl before use (Sikri, 2019).
- "There is no evidence on whether this step can affect the clinical outcome, [but] it should be considered good practice" (Azim and Aksel, 2022).
- Store cool and dry; the points become brittle in heat and humidity (Rotstein and Ingle, 2019).
- Coated cones and carrier obturators: follow the manufacturer's instructions, since disinfectants may affect coatings [Likely; not stated in the books read].

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count 121; about 6 min at 22 words/min)**

### Problem
Gutta-percha softens with heat, so autoclaving is impossible. Commercial cones are pre-sterilised but 5-8% are contaminated on removal from the pack and about 20% after opening.

### Methods
1. **5.25% sodium hypochlorite for 1 min** (chairside standard; kills spores), then rinse with alcohol or hydrogen peroxide.
2. 2% chlorhexidine for 5 min (Sikri) although Cohen's finds it ineffective against spores.
3. 10% PVP-iodine 30-60 s; paraformaldehyde vapour 1 h.
4. 2% glutaraldehyde and 70% ethanol do not kill spores.

### Points to state
Dip the notched master cone in NaOCl and dry before use; cold chemical disinfection only; store dry. No evidence yet that it changes outcome.

### Conclusion
Cold chemical disinfection with 5.25% NaOCl for one minute is the accepted method.
