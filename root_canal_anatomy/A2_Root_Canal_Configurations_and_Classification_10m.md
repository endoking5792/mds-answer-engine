## STUDY MODE

### S1. Meaning and basis [definition]
Variations are the norm (Grossman, 2021); a tapering canal with one foramen is the exception (Paqué, 2025).

### S2. Weine [classification]
Weine 2004 describes four types: I one canal; II two merging short of the apex; III two with separate foramina; IV one dividing (Weine, 2004). Sikri and Torabinejad et al code them (1-1), (2-1), (2-2), (1-2) (Sikri, 2019; Torabinejad et al, 2021). Torabinejad et al say the initial system had three types; Paqué says four, later five (Torabinejad et al, 2021; Paqué, 2025).

### S3. Vertucci [classification]
Grossman, Harty, Torabinejad et al and Paqué print eight Vertucci types; Weine 2004 says seven major types (Grossman, 2021; Harty, 2017; Torabinejad et al, 2021; Paqué, 2025; Weine, 2004). Type IV is (2) and VIII (3) in Grossman, (2-2) and (3-3) in Sikri and Torabinejad et al (Grossman, 2021; Sikri, 2019; Torabinejad et al, 2021). Dates: Grossman's caption prints Vertucci et al (1974) and 200 cleared maxillary second premolars (Grossman, 2021); Cohen's credit prints 1974 (Cohen, 2021); Harty and Paqué print 1984 (Harty, 2017; Paqué, 2025); the abstract of Vertucci (1984) reports 2,400 teeth (abstract only). Torabinejad et al suggest Type VIII for one broad or fused root, and note other studies classify three-rooted premolars as Type I in each root (Torabinejad et al, 2021).

### S4. Extended and newer systems [classification]
- Sikri lists Gulabivala et al types IX to XV (Sikri, 2019); Ingle gives Sert and Bayirli 14 types IX to XXIII (Ingle, year not printed); Cohen's figure panel lists others, e.g. (3-1), (4-2), (5-4) (Cohen, 2021).
- Micro-CT found 37 canal types (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- Ahmed et al coding: Sikri names Ahmed and Dummer (2017) and gives 215B1P2 for tooth, roots, canals (Sikri, 2019); Paqué gives tooth number, superscript root number and orifice-canal-foramen path, caption 2024 (Paqué, 2025); Cohen lists three systems, root and canal paper dated 2016 (Cohen, 2021). Web 2017 paper: snippet only. See P3.

### S5. Clinical relevance and anomalies [significance]
Single roots do not necessarily have single canals (Gulabivala and Ng, 2014); it is prudent to assume more than one canal system until proved otherwise (Torabinejad et al, 2021). Taurodont bleeding is not perforation (Ingle, year not printed). Gemination usually has one canal; fused teeth may have more than one canal system and are treated as one system (Ingle, year not printed).

## EXAM MODE
*(Answer-book version. Estimated writing time at 22 words per minute: about 18 minutes.)*

### 1. Introduction
Variation in canal configuration is the norm (Grossman, 2021). Weine (1969) gave the first clinical type system (Ingle, year not printed).

### 2. Weine classification
| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| I (1-1) | One canal, chamber to apex |
| II (2-1) | Two canals merge short of apex |
| III (2-2) | Two separate canals to apex |
| IV (1-2) | One canal divides into two |
(Grossman, 2021; Sikri, 2019; Torabinejad et al, 2021; Weine, 2004).

### 3. Vertucci classification
Eight types in Grossman, Harty, Torabinejad et al and Paqué; seven major types in Weine (Grossman, 2021; Harty, 2017; Torabinejad et al, 2021; Paqué, 2025; Weine, 2004). I: (1) Grossman, (1-1) Sikri and Torabinejad et al; II (2-1); III (1-2-1); IV two canals to apex: (2) Grossman, (2-2) Sikri and Torabinejad et al; V (1-2); VI (2-1-2); VII (1-2-1-2); VIII three canals to apex: (3) Grossman, (3-3) Sikri and Torabinejad et al (Grossman, 2021; Sikri, 2019; Torabinejad et al, 2021).

### 4. Extended and newer systems
- Gulabivala et al added types (Sikri, 2019); Sert and Bayirli added 14, IX to XXIII (Ingle, year not printed); Cohen's figure lists others (Cohen, 2021).
- Micro-CT: 37 types (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- Ahmed et al code tooth, roots, canals, e.g. 215B1P2 (Sikri, 2019); Paqué prints tooth number, roots, O-C-F path (Paqué, 2025). See P3.

### 5. Clinical relevance
- Single root does not mean single canal (Gulabivala and Ng, 2014); it is prudent to assume more than one canal system until proved otherwise (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- C-shaped canals are in A8 and extra roots in A9.

### 6. Dental anomalies
| Anomaly | Endodontic point |
|---|---|
| Taurodontism | Large chamber, apical floor; bleeding is not perforation (Torabinejad et al, 2021; Ingle, year not printed) |
| Dens invaginatus | Oehlers type 1 within crown (Torabinejad et al, 2021) |
| Dens evaginatus | Mainly Asian descent (Torabinejad et al, 2021) |
| Gemination, fusion | Gemination: usually one canal; fused teeth: treat as one system (Ingle, year not printed) |
| Dilaceration | Sharp root bend (Ingle, year not printed) |

### 7. Conclusion
Weine and Vertucci classify canals by number and joining; newer systems code more (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
