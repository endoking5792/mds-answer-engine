## STUDY MODE

### S1. Terms [definition]
AAE: an accessory canal is any branch of the main canal or chamber reaching the external root surface; a lateral canal is one in the coronal or middle third, usually horizontal; a furcation canal is one in the furcation; ramification means a gap from fragmentation of the epithelial root sheath, or unusual intracanal anatomy (AAE, 2012). Sikri: a lateral canal branches to the lateral surface, an accessory canal at the apical third or furcation (Sikri, 2019). De Deus (1975), as reported by Gulabivala and Ng, places lateral canals in the middle third and secondary canals in the apical region (Gulabivala and Ng, 2014).

### S2. Origin [aetiology]
Cohen: entrapment of periodontal vessels in Hertwig sheath (Cohen, 2021). Torabinejad et al: fragmentation of Hertwig sheath (Torabinejad et al, 2021).

### S3. Incidence [numbers as printed]
Incidence: Grossman 27.4 to 35.5% (Grossman, 2021); Sikri 27 to 35% (Sikri, 2019); Ingle 30.6% of teeth (Ingle, year not printed). By third: Cohen 74% apical, 11% middle, 15% cervical (Cohen, 2021); Sikri 70 to 75, 12 to 14, 15 to 18% (Sikri, 2019); Vertucci 73.5, 11.4, 6.3% (Torabinejad et al, 2021).

### S4. Significance [significance]
Irritants pass from pulp to periodontium (Cohen, 2021). Circulation: Cohen, "may not supply sufficient circulation" (Cohen, 2021); Sikri and Walton and Torabinejad, "do not supply collateral circulation" (Sikri, 2019; Walton and Torabinejad, 2002); Ingle, "serve as collateral circulation" (Ingle, year not printed). Cohen finds no evidence lateral canals must be obturated but says they should still be debrided and disinfected (Cohen, 2021); obturating them: importance unknown (Walton and Torabinejad, 2002). Weine: lateral canals in 38% of necrotic and 3% of vital pulps (Weine, 2004). Kim and Kratchman, cited in Ingle, suggest resecting at least 3 mm to include 98% of apical ramifications and 93% of lateral canals (Ingle, year not printed).

### S5. Isthmus [definition, classification, management]
An isthmus is a narrow ribbon-shaped communication between two canals (Cohen, 2021). Cohen's Kim types: I incomplete, II complete, III very short, IV three or more canals, V no connection (Cohen, 2021). Torabinejad et al's Hsu and Kim types: I no notable communication, II hair-thin connection, III three canals, IV extended canals, V true wide connection (Torabinejad et al, 2021). Complete disinfection is not possible (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
## EXAM MODE

### 1. Definitions
- Accessory canal: branch of the main canal or chamber reaching the external root surface (AAE, 2012).
- Lateral canal: in the coronal or middle third (AAE, 2012; Torabinejad et al, 2021); De Deus: middle third (Gulabivala and Ng, 2014).
- Furcation canal: in the furcation. Apical delta: main canal dividing into multiple accessory canals near the apex (AAE, 2012).

### 2. Origin
Cohen: entrapment of periodontal vessels in Hertwig sheath (Cohen, 2021). Torabinejad et al: fragmentation of Hertwig sheath (Torabinejad et al, 2021).

### 3. Incidence and location

### 4. Clinical significance
- Pathway for irritants, pulp to periodontium (Cohen, 2021).
- Collateral circulation: may not supply sufficient (Cohen, 2021); not supplied (Sikri, 2019; Walton and Torabinejad, 2002); supplied (Ingle, year not printed).
- Filling: no evidence it is needed, but disinfect (Cohen, 2021); importance unknown (Walton and Torabinejad, 2002).

### 5. Ramifications and apical delta
- Ramification: gap from fragmentation of the epithelial root sheath (AAE, 2012).
- Apical ramification: several portals of exit; 1 to 15.1% maxillary, 5 to 14% mandibular (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- Kim and Kratchman, cited in Ingle: resect at least 3 mm for 98% of ramifications and 93% of lateral canals (Ingle, year not printed).

### 6. Isthmus and its management
- Narrow ribbon communication between two canals (Cohen, 2021). Kim types, Cohen: I incomplete, II complete, III very short, IV three or more canals, V no connection (Cohen, 2021). Torabinejad et al: I none, II hair-thin, III three canals, IV extended canals, V wide (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- Suspect one when two canals are present (Sikri, 2019); use ultrasonic activation (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
- Surgery: include the isthmus in the root-end preparation (Cohen, 2021).

**Conclusion:** side canals usually cannot be instrumented, so disinfection relies on irrigation (Torabinejad et al, 2021).
