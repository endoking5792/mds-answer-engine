## EXAM MODE
*(Estimated writing time: ~16 minutes)*

### Definition
**AAE:** a surface film of debris retained on dentin or other surfaces after instrumentation with rotary instruments or endodontic files; consists of dentin particles, remnants of vital or necrotic pulp tissue, bacterial components and retained irrigants. First described by McComb and Smith (1975) by SEM.

### Structure
- Superficial layer about 1-2.5 μm (Cohen's 1-5 μm).
- Smear plugs in tubules up to about 40 μm.
- Contains bacteria and necrotic tissue, so differs from the coronal film.

### Formation
More with rotary than hand instruments, with dull instruments, and without irrigation (up to 70% more); least with sonic/ultrasonic preparation.

### Significance
| Retain | Remove (favoured by most) |
|---|---|
| Blocks tubules, lowers permeability | May be infected, protects bacteria in tubules |
| Barrier to bacterial migration | Blocks irrigants and medicaments |
| Removal may allow reinfection | Prevents sealer penetration and adhesion |
| Chelators erode dentin | Loose, dissolves, leaves a void and a leakage path |
Laboratory evidence is mixed (26 studies: 53.8% no difference, 41.5% favour removal, 4.7% favour retention); no clinical outcome trial; no consensus.

### Removal
- NaOCl cannot remove the inorganic part; EDTA alone cannot remove the organic part.
- **17% EDTA about 1 minute alternating with NaOCl** is the method of choice; weaker in the apical third; 10 minutes erodes dentin.
- Alternatives: MTAD, QMix, citric acid, SmearClear.
- Activation: ultrasonic, laser (Er:YAG better than Nd:YAG).

### Effect on obturation
Sealer enters tubules only when the layer is removed; better adaptation of gutta-percha.


**Note:** EDTA is self-limiting (it stops once all chelator is bound to calcium), so correct contact time rather than longer time is the aim; the layer re-forms after any later instrumentation.

### Conclusion
Remove in infected canals with EDTA and NaOCl; benefit for healing is unproven.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
AAE definition; 1-2.5 μm and 40 μm plugs; EDTA + NaOCl; no consensus.
