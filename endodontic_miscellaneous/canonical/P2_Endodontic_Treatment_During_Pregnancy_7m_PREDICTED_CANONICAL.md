# Endodontic treatment during pregnancy: radiographs, anaesthesia, drugs and timing

**Marks:** 7 · Short essay (predicted)

Pregnancy is not a reason to deny treatment (Wynne, 2025). Odontogenic infection should be treated promptly in any trimester (Wynne, 2025); endodontic therapy for pain or infection is given regardless of trimester (Johnson and Epstein, 2019). Consult the obstetrician (Wynne, 2025).

| Aspect | Management |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | The first 3 months are vital to fetal growth (Wynne, 2025); avoid elective care then (Rosenberg and Malek, 2021). The second trimester is ideal; extensive elective work waits until after delivery (Wynne, 2025). Late: short visits, left lateral position, right hip raised 15 degrees (Wynne, 2025). |
| **Radiographs** | Intraoral films are safe with high-speed film, collimation, filtration, lead apron and thyroid collar; take only those needed (Wynne, 2025). No increase in anomalies below 5-10 cGy (Basrani, 2012). The risk of omitting a needed film may exceed that of the radiation (Rosenberg and Malek, 2021). |
| **Local anaesthesia** | Lidocaine and prilocaine are FDA category B, first choice; bupivacaine, mepivacaine and articaine are C (Wynne, 2025). Epinephrine with aspiration, not above 0.04 mg (Wynne, 2025). |
| **Antibiotics** | Penicillins, cephalosporins and macrolides first line (Rosenberg and Malek, 2021); avoid tetracycline, minocycline, doxycycline (Wynne, 2025). |
| **Analgesics** | Acetaminophen first line; aspirin not recommended; ibuprofen contraindicated in the third trimester (Wynne, 2025). |
| **Anxiolysis** | Non-pharmacological methods preferred; benzodiazepines are category C or D (Wynne, 2025). |
| **Materials** | Hypochlorite and root filling materials are not detrimental to the fetus (Wynne, 2025). |

Books differ. Analgesics: Rosenberg and Malek (2021) report British advice against ibuprofen from the 30th week and a possible infant respiratory link with acetaminophen later in pregnancy. Nitrous oxide: controversial (Wynne, 2025); one exposure under 35 minutes accepted (Rosenberg and Malek, 2021).
