# Elaborate on biological properties of endodontic materials.

**Marks:** 10 · Long essay

**Answer blueprint:** Introduction → Material-wise biological properties → Sealing and dimensional properties → Book differences → Conclusion

## Introduction
Endodontic materials contact periapical tissue, so they must be **biocompatible**, causing no toxic, allergic, inflammatory or carcinogenic reaction (Sikri, 2019). An ideal sealer is also at least bacteriostatic (van Noort, 2013).

## Biological properties by material
| Material | Biocompatibility and tissue response | Antimicrobial and bioactive properties |
|---|---|---|
| **Zinc oxide-eugenol sealers** | Mild cytotoxicity, usually not clinically significant (Sikri, 2019); marked cytotoxicity in cell culture yet clinical success, as cultures lack in vivo defences; resorb if extruded (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) | Antimicrobial (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) |
| **Calcium hydroxide sealers** | Osteogenic response not demonstrated (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) | Mildly antibacterial, ineffective against E. faecalis (Sikri, 2019); not demonstrated (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) |
| **Bioceramic sealers** | Established as biocompatible (Sikri, 2019); EndoSequence BC was more cytotoxic than AH Plus, taking 5 weeks to become non-cytotoxic against 3 (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) | (not stated) |
| **Gutta-percha** | Minimal toxicity (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) | Added antimicrobials not shown effective (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) |
| **MTA** | Biocompatible; induces hard tissue formation (Aminoshariae et al, 2021); pulp capping gives reparative dentin (Nikhil et al, 2024) | Antibacterial against E. faecalis, but one study found no action against anaerobes (Nikhil et al, 2024) |
| **Irrigants and medicaments** | Sodium hypochlorite is cytotoxic; chlorhexidine has low tissue toxicity (Peters et al, 2021); EDTA is biocompatible clinically (Aminoshariae et al, 2021); formocresol is highly toxic, mutagenic and carcinogenic (Peters et al, 2021) | Calcium hydroxide dressing: less effective against E. faecalis and C. albicans (Aminoshariae et al, 2021) |

Bioceramics are generally less cytotoxic, though Haglund found MTA cytotoxic to macrophages and fibroblasts, subsiding with time; calcium phosphate bioceramics are bioactive, precipitating biological apatite (Nikhil et al, 2024).

## Sealing and dimensional properties
- Solubility should not exceed 3.0% by mass; MTA-Fillapex and Root SP show 15 to 20% (Sikri, 2019).
- Bioceramic sealers bond and adapt better; others seal comparably (Sikri, 2019).
- Gutta-percha lacks adhesion and, heated, shrinks on cooling (Aminoshariae et al, 2021); no sealer gives an impervious seal (van Noort, 2013).

## Where books differ
Sikri (2019) calls bioceramic sealers biocompatible, while Aminoshariae et al (2021) report one as initially more cytotoxic than AH Plus. Zinc oxide-eugenol cytotoxicity is mild for Sikri but marked in culture for Aminoshariae et al.
