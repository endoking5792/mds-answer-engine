## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the grouping into timing/radiographs/anaesthesia/antibiotics/analgesics/anxiolysis is mine. "Late: short visits, left lateral position" paraphrases Wynne's third-trimester paragraph (short appointments, positioning to prevent supine hypotension, left lateral decubitus with right hip elevated 15 degrees).
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) First-trimester care: Cohen says elective care is best avoided in the first trimester; Ingle says elective procedures may often be done in the second; Wynne says the second trimester is ideal. Consistent (elective care in the second trimester), shown as such. (2) Analgesics: Wynne names acetaminophen first line; Cohen reports a possible infant respiratory link with acetaminophen in middle to late pregnancy (not validated, confounding questioned) and the 2016 British advice against ibuprofen from the 30th week (and earlier avoidance); Wynne bars ibuprofen only in the third trimester. (3) Nitrous oxide: Wynne controversial, category-avoid in first trimester; Cohen single exposure under 35 minutes not associated with fetal anomalies. (4) Drug classification: Ingle and Wynne use FDA letters A-X; Cohen notes FDA changed to narrative labelling in 2014 (p2_coh_fda); the answer follows Wynne. (5) Wynne puts the first trimester at conception to the 14th week; not used.
