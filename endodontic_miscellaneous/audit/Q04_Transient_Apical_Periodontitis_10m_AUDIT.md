## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** the opening definition ('a short-lived, self-limiting inflammation of the apical periodontal ligament') is the answer's own synthesis, not a textbook definition: no textbook consulted defines 'transient apical periodontitis'. The only textbook uses are Grossman 14e Ch.21 ('transient apical periodontal inflammation', as the usual cause, with occlusal disturbance, of postendodontic discomfort) and the unrelated 'transient apical breakdown' (AAE Glossary; Andreasen's 1986 paper is only listed in reference lists, Ingle/Torabinejad Ch.11). The answer therefore maps the exam term onto Grossman's 'primary symptomatic apical periodontitis' (formerly acute apical periodontitis) and Sikri's 'acute apical periodontitis'; this mapping is interpretation and is stated as such in the text.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the Diagnosis paragraph's two distinctions (no radiolucency versus secondary symptomatic apical periodontitis; no swelling versus acute apical abscess) are from Grossman 14e Ch.5 (the second is Grossman's phrase 'extreme clinical symptoms of swelling and pain ... breakdown of periradicular tissue').
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) Causes: Grossman 14e lists overinstrumentation, forced debris or irrigant, extruded filling as causes of primary symptomatic apical periodontitis; Torabinejad 6e (Walton, Ruparel) states that no definitive treatment risk factors for flare-ups have been identified, though overextension has the highest postobturation discomfort. Both shown. (2) Occlusal reduction: Cohen 19 - not beneficial in vital teeth not initially percussion sensitive, reported to reduce pain when percussion pain was present; Gulabivala and Ng - only after proper occlusal assessment, not routine; Grossman - adjust high points. Shown. (3) Pain mechanism (Ingle 6: compression of nerve endings versus mediators; Cohen 16: mechanical allodynia) not included for length.
- **Numerical claims:** none beyond the 'few days to a week' duration (Grossman 14e Ch.21); the marks figure is the only other number.
