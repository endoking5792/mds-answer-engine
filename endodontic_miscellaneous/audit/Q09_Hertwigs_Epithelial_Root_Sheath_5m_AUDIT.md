## Second-reader audit U4 (see audit/AUDIT_u4.md)

- **Corrected:** 'Trauma ... causes partial arrest of root formation' overstated Andreasen (the trauma has 'apparently directly injured' the sheath, compromising root development; foreshortening is the radiographic finding, u4_and_short). Reworded and the 'causes' dropped. The Ch.12 immature-apex bullet removed (above).
- **Added (answer):** C-shaped canals from failure of the sheath to fuse on the buccal or lingual root surface (Gutmann and Fan, Cohen Ch.7, u4_coh7_cshape).
- **Conflict shown, not ranked:** accessory canals form by a sheath defect (Gopikrishna q09_gr_acc), by periodontal vessels entrapped in the sheath (Cohen Ch.7, u4_coh7_acc), or both (Walton 2002, u4_wal_lat1 and u4_wal_lat2). The answer states the first and says 'a sheath defect leaves a gap'; the vessel-entrapment version is in the sidecar only.
- **Sidecar only (cut for length):** sheath pattern regulates root width, curvature, length and number (Walton 2002, u4_wal_pat); innermost sheath cells secrete a hyaline material that becomes the hyaline layer of Hopewell-Smith (u4_wal_hyal); pulp stones near the apex form around sheath remnants (Cohen Ch.13, u4_coh13_stone); palatal radicular groove is an infolding of inner enamel epithelium and sheath (Sturdevant, u4_stu_groove; Ingle ch.1 'deep radicular groove', u4_ing1_groove); Marzouk gives loss of sheath continuity as programmed cell death (not located).

- **Open point:** Cohen Ch.7 (Gutmann, Fan; authors from the chapter header) and Ch.13 (Fristad, Berggreen) have PDF page markers only.
