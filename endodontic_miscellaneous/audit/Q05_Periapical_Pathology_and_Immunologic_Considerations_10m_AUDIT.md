## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator. The last sentence of the answer ('Healing therefore depends on eliminating the canal infection') is the answer's own reasoning from Cohen 16 ('inadequate to eliminate these factors') and Torabinejad 6e (bacteria in persistent lesions); the TH1/TH2/TH17 table condenses Ingle 6 and is attributed to it.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** 'macrophages and complement act first' follows Ingle 6 ('resident macrophages and the complement system'); 'neutrophils the first leukocytes to arrive' is Ingle 6, 'principal effector cells' is Cohen 16.
- **Conflicting sources:** no true conflict. Torabinejad 6e: initial phases of pulpitis and apical periodontitis are coordinated mainly by innate responses, adaptive responses prevent dissemination of virulent infection; Cohen 16: acute = innate, chronic = adaptive; Ingle 6: asymptomatic apical periodontitis = adaptive response. Consistent. Ingle 6 gives granuloma, cyst and abscess definitions that differ slightly from Cohen 16 (Cohen: a granuloma is relatively avascular and the term is used interchangeably with chronic apical periodontitis, not all lesions show granulomatous features); not shown for length.
- **Numerical claims:** about 50% of persistent lesions contain bacteria (Torabinejad 6e Ch.1, q05_tor1_pers). No other numbers.
- **Literature evidence:** none opened; knockout-mouse and cytokine findings are known only through the textbook accounts.
