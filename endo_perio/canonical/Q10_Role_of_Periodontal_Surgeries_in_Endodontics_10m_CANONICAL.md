## Explanation

The question is about the periodontal procedures an endodontist uses or relies on. Cohen's frames them as alternatives when routine endodontic and periodontal treatment fail: resection techniques remove diseased roots, regenerative techniques rebuild lost structures (Glickman and Iacono, 2021) [SRC]. Kinaia et al give the biology behind the regenerative side: Melcher's four cell types, of which only ligament and bone cells regenerate attachment, so a barrier that excludes epithelium allows new attachment; bone grafts, by contrast, mostly heal by repair (Kinaia et al, 2012) [SRC]. In endodontic surgery, GTR adds benefit for large through-and-through lesions but not small confined ones (Kinaia et al, 2012) [SRC]. Growth factors such as PDGF and IGF help periodontal defects but give controversial results in endodontic sites, and BMP-2 has caused ankylosis (Kinaia et al, 2012) [SRC]. Gulabivala and Ng warn the cooperation runs both ways: root canal treatment done "to be safe" before regenerative surgery can harm the outcome if material is extruded (Gulabivala, Darbar and Ng, 2014) [SRC].

**Regeneration.** Kinaia et al trace the history: early surgical treatments (including root amputation and hemisection) healed by long junctional epithelium; bone grafts mostly by repair; GTR is based on Melcher's idea that only ligament and bone cells regenerate attachment (Kinaia et al, 2012) [SRC]. For endodontic surgery a systematic review found GTR helps large communicating (through-and-through) lesions but not small confined ones (Kinaia et al, 2012) [SRC]. Cohen's adds that with GTR the combined endodontic-periodontal lesion probably has the least favourable prognosis (Glickman and Iacono, 2021) [SRC].

## ✎ Exam core

**Introduction.** Periodontal surgery helps where non-surgical treatment cannot reach the cause, and in endo-perio lesions (Glickman and Iacono, 2021).

**1. Diagnostic (exploratory) flap**
- To confirm a vertical root fracture or determine the cause of a lesion when the diagnosis is uncertain (Rotstein, 2017).

**2. Resective procedures** (Glickman and Iacono, 2021)
- Root amputation, hemisection, bicuspidization for molars with one root lost to periodontitis, fracture, perforation, resorption or an untreatable canal.
- Endodontics first: vital resected roots lose vitality (38% vital at 1 year, 13% at 5 years).
- Success 62% to 100%.

**3. Regenerative procedures**
- **GTR**: a membrane excludes epithelium so that ligament and bone cells repopulate the defect (Melcher) (Kinaia et al, 2012).
- In endodontic surgery, GTR benefits large through-and-through lesions, not small confined ones; resorbable membranes avoid a second surgery (Kinaia et al, 2012; Glickman and Iacono, 2021).
- Periradicular healing is faster at membrane sites (Glickman and Iacono, 2021).
- Combined endo-perio lesions have the least favourable prognosis with GTR (Glickman and Iacono, 2021).
- **Bone grafts**: autograft (osteogenic), allograft (osteoinductive), xenograft and alloplast (osteoconductive); healing mainly by repair (Kinaia et al, 2012).
- **Enamel matrix derivative and growth factors**: promising periodontally, inconsistent in endodontic sites; BMP-2 linked to ankylosis (Kinaia et al, 2012).

**4. Perforation and resorption repair**
- Perforation near the alveolar crest: raise a flap, repair with MTA or another material, reposition the flap (Rotstein, 2017; Rotstein, Zabalegui and Al-Hezaimi, 2019).
- Prognosis depends on size, location, time to repair and the material (Rotstein, Zabalegui and Al-Hezaimi, 2019).

**5. Palatogingival (radicular) groove**
- Scaling and root planing alone will not work; flap, remove the groove with a bur, place a bone substitute (Rotstein, Zabalegui and Al-Hezaimi, 2019); early treatment improves prognosis (Rotstein, 2017).

**6. Exposing sound tooth structure**
- Surgical crown lengthening or, as an alternative, orthodontic extrusion; most successful extrusions are under 4 mm, retained twice as long as the extrusion took (Glickman and Iacono, 2021).

**7. Periodontal therapy in endo-perio lesions**
- After root canal treatment and reassessment at 2 to 3 months, residual defects receive periodontal therapy, including surgery (Rotstein, Zabalegui and Al-Hezaimi, 2019).

**Conclusion.** Periodontal surgery extends endodontic treatment by allowing diagnosis of fractures, removal of untreatable roots, repair of perforations and grooves, and regeneration of lost attachment, but complete regeneration is not yet predictable (Kinaia et al, 2012).

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Resection and regeneration are the two families (Cohen's).
- GTR helps large through-and-through lesions only.
- Endodontics before root resection.
