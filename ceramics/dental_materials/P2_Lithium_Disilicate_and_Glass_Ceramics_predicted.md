## STUDY MODE
**Composition and microstructure.** About 65-70 vol% elongated interlocking lithium disilicate (Li2Si2O5) crystals in a glass matrix, with lithium orthophosphate (Li3PO4) (Nejat, 2025; Zhang and Kelly, 2017). Press crystals are about 4 μm long and aligned; CAD crystals about 1 μm and random; Press has slightly higher toughness, CAD slightly higher strength (Zhang and Kelly, 2017).

**Forms — processing specifics confirmed from Craig's, read directly this session.** Heat-pressed at **910-920°C** (CR)/920°C (VN, lower than leucite's 1180°C) [SRC, CR/VN]. CAD blocks are machined in a **partially-crystallised state** (lithium metasilicate + lithium disilicate nuclei), then crystallised by a **post-mill firing at 850°C for 10 minutes** (CR) — a more specific figure than the previously-cited "840°C for 7 min"; both figures likely reflect different manufacturer protocols, so cite CR's figure with its source [SRC, CR]. A **zirconia-containing lithium silicate variant** exists with ~10 wt% ZrO2, crystallised at 840°C/8min (CR) [SRC, CR]. Fully crystallised blocks needing no firing (n!ce, GC Initial LiSi); virgilite blocks with one glaze firing (CEREC Tessera); nano-crystal blocks (Amber Mill) (Nejat, 2025).

**Microstructure, confirmed (Craig's).** The final crystallised microstructure is **~65 vol% interlocking prismatic crystals, 2-5 µm length × 0.8 µm diameter, with ~1 vol% residual porosity** — a more specific figure than the earlier "about 65-70 vol%" approximation [SRC, CR].

**Strengthening.** Dispersion strengthening and crack bridging/deflection; thermal-expansion mismatch puts the glass in compression around crystals (Nejat, 2025).

**Fracture toughness and strength — genuinely edition-dependent, do not state one figure as settled.** Craig's explicitly classifies **Vita Suprinity/Celtra Duo as lithium SILICATE** (Li2Si2O5 + Li2SiO3, **>50% crystallinity, ~420 MPa**) as **distinct from pure lithium DISILICATE** (65% crystallinity, **262±88 MPa hard-machined / 306±29 MPa heat-pressed** per CR; van Noort gives **350-450 MPa**; CR's own heat-pressed manufacturer figure elsewhere is **360-400 MPa**) — if lithium disilicate and lithium silicate are being used interchangeably anywhere in this answer, that needs correcting [SRC, CR/VN]. The fracture-toughness ladder is also edition-dependent: Craig's gives soda-lime glass→leucite-reinforced→lithium disilicate as 0.78→1.2→2.75 MPa·m^0.5; Phillips' 12th ed. gives 0.75→1.3→3.3 MPa·m^0.5; Michigan's older data gives lithium disilicate 3.2 MPa·m^0.5 specifically — cite the edition with the figure [SRC, CR/MICH].

**Properties.** 350-530 MPa depending on source and method (Moshaverinia 350; Zhang and Kelly 400-480; Bacchi 450-550; Nejat 530 manufacturer; CR's own figures given above by crystallinity/processing route); translucency adjustable by block type (HT, LT, HO).

**Indications.** Veneers, inlays/onlays, anterior and posterior crowns, 3-unit bridges to second premolar (manufacturer); long-term data support single restorations anywhere in the mouth and short-span anterior FDPs; caution for FDPs (Zhang and Kelly, 2017; Nejat, 2025).

**Bonding.** HF etch 4.6% 20 s (Blatz) or 5% 20-30 s (Nejat manufacturer), silane, resin cement.

**Advantages and limitations.** Strength with esthetics; press or CAD; bondable; versatile. Limitations: FDP failure at connectors; limited masking; wear of antagonist if rough; milled version has lower fatigue than pressed in some studies (Bacchi and Cesar, 2022).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 300)**

### Definition
Glass-ceramic with about 65-70 vol% lithium disilicate crystals in a glass matrix (CR: ~65 vol%, 2-5µm×0.8µm interlocking prisms, ~1vol% residual porosity). Not interchangeable with "lithium silicate" (Vita Suprinity/Celtra Duo: Li2Si2O5+Li2SiO3, >50% crystallinity, ~420 MPa, CR) — a genuinely different, lower-crystallinity material.

### Composition and structure
SiO2-Li2O-Al2O3-K2O-P2O5-ZrO2; interlocking needle crystals.

### Forms
| Form | Processing |
|---|---|
| e.max Press | Ingot pressed at 910-920°C (CR/VN) |
| e.max CAD | Partially-crystallised block milled, crystallised by post-mill firing 850°C/10min (CR) |
| Zirconia-containing lithium silicate | ~10wt% ZrO2, crystallised 840°C/8min (CR) |
| Fully crystallised blocks | No firing needed |

### Properties
Strength 350-530 MPa (method-dependent). Edition-attributed: CR gives lithium disilicate 262±88 MPa hard-machined/306±29 MPa heat-pressed; VN gives 350-450 MPa; lithium SILICATE (Suprinity/Celtra, distinct material) ~420 MPa (CR). Toughness ladder: CR 2.75, PHIL 3.3, MICH (older) 3.2 MPa·m^0.5 — edition-dependent, cite source. Translucency variants (HT/LT/HO).

### Indications
Veneers, inlays, onlays, crowns anywhere, 3-unit anterior or to second premolar.

### Bonding
HF + silane + resin cement.

### Advantages
Strength, esthetics, bondable, press/CAD choices.

### Limitations
Not for long spans; moderate masking; antagonist wear if rough.

### Conclusion
Lithium disilicate is the most versatile glass-ceramic for single units.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- ~65 vol% crystals (CR); KIc edition-dependent: CR 2.75, PHIL 3.3, MICH 3.2.
- Press vs CAD crystals; CAD blocks machined partially-crystallised, then post-mill fired (850°C/10min, CR).
- Lithium SILICATE (Suprinity/Celtra, >50% crystallinity, ~420 MPa) ≠ lithium DISILICATE (65% crystallinity, strength varies by source/processing) — do not conflate the terms.
- HF 20 s + silane.
