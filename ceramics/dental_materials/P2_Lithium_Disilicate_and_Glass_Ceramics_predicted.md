## STUDY MODE
**Composition and microstructure.** About 65-70 vol% elongated interlocking lithium disilicate (Li2Si2O5) crystals in a glass matrix, with lithium orthophosphate (Li3PO4) (Nejat, 2025; Zhang and Kelly, 2017). Press crystals are about 4 μm long and aligned; CAD crystals about 1 μm and random; Press has slightly higher toughness, CAD slightly higher strength (Zhang and Kelly, 2017).

**Forms.**
- Press ingots: pressed at 920 °C for 20 min (Zhang and Kelly, 2017).
- CAD blocks: precrystallised lithium metasilicate (about 130 MPa) milled, then crystallised (above 750 °C with P2O5 nucleator in Nejat; 840 °C for 7 min in Zhang and Kelly).
- Fully crystallised blocks needing no firing (n!ce, GC Initial LiSi); virgilite blocks with one glaze firing (CEREC Tessera); nano-crystal blocks (Amber Mill) (Nejat, 2025).

**Strengthening.** Dispersion strengthening and crack bridging/deflection; thermal-expansion mismatch puts the glass in compression around crystals (Nejat, 2025); fracture toughness about 3.3 MPa·m^0.5 at 70 vol% (Anusavice et al, 2013, via digest).

**Properties.** 350-530 MPa depending on source and method (Moshaverinia 350; Zhang and Kelly 400-480; Bacchi 450-550; Nejat 530 manufacturer); translucency adjustable by block type (HT, LT, HO).

**Indications.** Veneers, inlays/onlays, anterior and posterior crowns, 3-unit bridges to second premolar (manufacturer); long-term data support single restorations anywhere in the mouth and short-span anterior FDPs; caution for FDPs (Zhang and Kelly, 2017; Nejat, 2025).

**Bonding.** HF etch 4.6% 20 s (Blatz) or 5% 20-30 s (Nejat manufacturer), silane, resin cement.

**Advantages and limitations.** Strength with esthetics; press or CAD; bondable; versatile. Limitations: FDP failure at connectors; limited masking; wear of antagonist if rough; milled version has lower fatigue than pressed in some studies (Bacchi and Cesar, 2022).

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 300)**

### Definition
Glass-ceramic with about 70 vol% lithium disilicate crystals in a glass matrix.

### Composition and structure
SiO2-Li2O-Al2O3-K2O-P2O5-ZrO2; interlocking needle crystals.

### Forms
| Form | Processing |
|---|---|
| e.max Press | Ingot pressed at 920 °C |
| e.max CAD | Precrystallised block milled, crystallised (about 840 °C, 7 min) |
| Fully crystallised blocks | No firing needed |

### Properties
Strength 350-530 MPa (method-dependent); toughness 2.3-3.3 MPa·m^0.5; translucency variants.

### Indications
Veneers, inlays, onlays, crowns anywhere, 3-unit anterior or to second premolar.

### Bonding
HF + silane + resin cement.

### Advantages
Strength, esthetics, bondable, press/CAD choices.

### Limitations
Not for long spans; moderate masking; antagonist wear if rough.

### Conclusion
Lithium disilicate is the most versatile glass-ceramic for single units.
