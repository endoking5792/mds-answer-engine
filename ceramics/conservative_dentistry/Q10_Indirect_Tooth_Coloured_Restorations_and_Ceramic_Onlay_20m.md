## STUDY MODE

### Indirect tooth-coloured restorations
Ceramic: inlay, onlay, veneer, crown (full or partial), partial-coverage ("overlay"/occlusal veneer), resin-bonded bridge, endocrown; composite: indirect inlay, onlay, veneer, crown; resin-matrix and PICN; gold is not tooth-coloured but may be mentioned as a palatal veneer (Sikri, 2017; Ricketts and Bartlett, 2011, via digest; Nejat, 2025).

### Treatment planning
Indication (loss of more than half of tooth structure, wide proximal area, cusp weakness, root-filled posterior tooth), occlusal analysis (contacts, parafunction), enamel availability for bonding, margin position relative to gingiva, material choice by load and esthetics (Sikri, 2017, via digest; Moshaverinia, 2020).

### Preparation of a ceramic onlay
- **Path of insertion** single, opposite to occlusal load; opposing axial walls slightly convergent (about 2-5° from the path in Marzouk; about 10° total taper target for resin-luted restorations in Sikri) (via digest).
- **Wall height** at least 3 mm; molar 4 mm; undercuts removed or blocked (Sikri, 2017, via digest).
- **Occlusal reduction** uniform and following cusp inclines; manufacturer minimum thickness 1.0 mm (lithium disilicate) to 1.5 mm (feldspathic, leucite, ZLS); inlay/onlay 1.0-1.5 mm depth (Nejat, 2025; Sikri, 2017, via digest). Working cusps are reduced more than non-working cusps for metal onlays (1.0-1.5 vs 0.5-1.0 mm) (Sikri, 2017, via digest); ceramic needs at least the manufacturer minimum.
- **Cuspal coverage** when a cusp is undermined or has less than adequate dentin support [Likely].
- **Margins:** butt-joint, no bevel on enamel, finish line in sound tooth, avoiding the occluso-buccal line angle of mandibular teeth and occluso-palatal of maxillary teeth where cusp coverage is indicated (Sikri, 2017, via digest). Rounded line angles (stress concentration; laboratory fabrication). Margin placement relative to gingiva: supragingival where possible (Sikri, 2017, via digest).
- **Deep caries or missing walls:** build-up (adhesive base/composite) to remove undercuts and create a favourable shape [Likely].

### Impression procedures
Elastomeric (addition silicone or polyether) putty-wash or monophase; custom or stock tray; gingival retraction for subgingival margins; or intraoral scanning. Provisional restoration made after impression. Details (materials and techniques): see Impression Materials topic (Q2 Elastomers, Q3 impressions for multiple inlays, Q4 digital impressions). Impression requirements: accurate margins, no voids, adequate retraction, dry field [Likely].

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 650; about 30 min)**

### A. Indirect tooth-coloured restorations
| Material | Restorations |
|---|---|
| Ceramic | Inlay, onlay, veneer, partial/full crown, endocrown, resin-bonded bridge |
| Indirect composite | Inlay, onlay, veneer, crown |
| Resin-matrix ceramic (PICN, nanoceramic) | Inlay, onlay, veneer, crown (CAD/CAM) |
Fabrication: laboratory (sintered, pressed) or CAD/CAM (chairside or lab).

### B. Treatment planning
Indications: more than half of crown lost; wide proximal box; weakened cusps; root-filled molar; esthetic demand. Check: occlusion, enamel for bonding, margins, isolation, patient factors.

### C. Preparation for ceramic onlay
1. Single path of insertion along the long axis.
2. Axial walls convergent about 10° (resin-luted), height at least 3 mm.
3. Remove undercuts and old restorations; adhesive build-up if needed.
4. Uniform occlusal reduction 1.0-1.5 mm, following cuspal anatomy; functional cusp covered when weak.
5. Butt-joint, no bevel; margins in sound enamel, supragingival if possible; avoid heavy occlusal contacts at the margin.
6. Rounded internal and external line angles.
7. Proximal boxes with open, smooth walls; contacts.
8. Check clearance in centric and excursions; finish.

### D. Impression procedures
- Gingival retraction (cord or paste) for margins near gingiva.
- Elastomeric material: putty-wash or monophase with addition silicone or polyether; custom or stock tray.
- Or digital scan.
- Opposing arch impression, occlusal record, shade.
- Provisional: bis-acryl or composite with temporary cement (non-eugenol).
- Laboratory prescription: material, shade, characterisation.

### E. Cementation
Rubber dam; HF-etch and silane the intaglio; phosphoric acid and adhesive on tooth; dual-cure resin cement.

### Conclusion
Ceramic onlays need a simple, well-finished, single-path preparation with adequate thickness and a precise impression, followed by adhesive cementation.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Taper about 10° for resin-luted indirect; wall height 3 mm (molar 4 mm).
- No bevel on ceramic margins.
- Thickness 1.0-1.5 mm.
- Non-eugenol provisional cement.
