## STUDY MODE
Preparation aims to create space for ceramic of adequate thickness, within enamel, with a clean finish line, rounded angles and a path of insertion. Principles (Sikri, 2017, via digest): keep the preparation in enamel because bonding to dentin is less reliable; if dentin is exposed widely, convert to a crown.

Thickness by material, manufacturer minimums (Nejat, 2025): feldspathic cervical 0.2-0.3, middle 0.5, incisal 0.5-0.7 mm; leucite 0.6/0.7/0.7 mm; lithium disilicate 0.4-0.6 / 0.5-0.6 / 0.5-0.7 mm; ZLS 0.4/0.6/1.0-1.5 mm; 3Y zirconia 0.4 mm.

Generic ceramic principles: rounded internal line angles, uniform reduction, chamfer or 1.0 mm margin for full coverage (Ricketts and Bartlett, 2011; Sikri, 2017, via digest).

**The full veneer-specific preparation technique, now filling the previous gap (SIKRI; STURD):**

**SIKRI's LVS (Laminate Veneer System) kit and 6-step sequence.** Depth-cutting burs: **LVS-1** (0.3 mm depth, cervical orientation grooves), **LVS-2** (0.5 mm depth, incisal/middle-third grooves), **LVS-3/LVS-4** (proximal chamfer burs). Steps: (1) labial reduction via depth grooves then reduction between them; (2) interproximal reduction/extension; (3) gingival/sulcular extension; (4) finish-line establishment; (5) incisal reduction, with 3 design choices — chamfer/incisal-overlap, butt-joint, or lingual-wrap/window; (6) lingual reduction only if the incisal design needs it. Temporisation is usually unnecessary for an enamel-confined prep [SRC, SIKRI].

**STURD's independent protocol.** Facial reduction **0.5-0.75 mm mid-facial, tapering to 0.3-0.5 mm gingivally**, cut in **3 zones** verified by **diamond-striation merging**; incisal reduction **1 mm minimum, 1.5 mm preferred**, gauged with a **silicone index**. Bur sequence: **tapered rounded-end diamond (Ø1-1.2 mm)** as its own depth gauge → **flame-shaped fine diamond** for the gingival finish line → **30-fluted carbide finishing bur used dry (Midwest #9803/Brasseler #7801)** → **Dialite Porcelain Polishing Kit** with porcelain polishing paste [SRC, STURD].

## EXAM MODE
**(Word count about 330; about 15 min)**

### Aim
Create space for ceramic, preserve enamel, give a smooth finish line and a path of insertion.

### Principles
- Stay in enamel (bond to enamel).
- Reduce according to material and third of the tooth.
- Rounded internal angles; no undercuts; smooth margins.
- Retain contact where possible; preserve the incisal edge unless length or masking is needed.

### Reduction (minimum, manufacturer data)
| Zone | Feldspathic | Lithium disilicate |
|---|---|---|
| Cervical | 0.2-0.3 mm | 0.4-0.6 mm |
| Middle | 0.5 mm | 0.5-0.6 mm |
| Incisal | 0.5-0.7 mm | 0.5-0.7 mm |

### Steps (SIKRI's LVS 6-step sequence; STURD's protocol corroborates and extends)
1. Diagnostic wax-up and silicone index.
2. Depth orientation grooves with LVS-1 (0.3 mm, cervical) and LVS-2 (0.5 mm, incisal/middle) depth-cutting burs; STURD's equivalent uses a tapered rounded-end diamond (Ø1-1.2 mm) as its own depth gauge, cutting 3 zones verified by diamond-striation merging.
3. Labial reduction in cervical and incisal planes: STURD gives 0.5-0.75 mm mid-facial tapering to 0.3-0.5 mm gingivally.
4. Interproximal reduction/extension; gingival/sulcular extension; finish-line establishment (flame-shaped fine diamond for gingival dressing — STURD).
5. Incisal reduction according to design (1 mm minimum, 1.5 mm preferred — STURD, gauged with a silicone index).
6. Lingual reduction only if the incisal design requires palatal coverage (SIKRI).
7. Finishing with a 30-fluted carbide bur used dry (Midwest #9803/Brasseler #7801), then the Dialite Porcelain Polishing Kit (STURD); refine margin; check through the silicone index.
8. Impression; provisional usually unnecessary for an enamel-confined prep (SIKRI) — if needed, fabricated and placed simultaneously intraorally with only a 2 mm spot-etch, not a full-surface bond (STURD).

### Designs
Window/lingual-wrap (incisal edge preserved on natural tooth, most conservative, least retentive), butt-joint (ceramic meets enamel at the edge, simplest), chamfer/incisal-overlap (ceramic wraps the incisal edge and a few mm of palatal — strongest, best for lengthening) — chosen by need for length, strength and masking (SIKRI).

### Precautions
Avoid dentin exposure; avoid sharp angles; preserve gingival health; consider tooth colour and masking need.

### Conclusion
Veneer preparation is minimal and enamel-confined, with depth set by material and zone, and design chosen for strength and esthetics.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Stay in enamel.
- Reduction by third and material; STURD: 0.5-0.75mm mid-facial → 0.3-0.5mm gingival; incisal 1mm min/1.5mm preferred.
- SIKRI LVS kit: LVS-1 0.3mm cervical, LVS-2 0.5mm incisal; STURD bur sequence: Ø1-1.2mm tapered diamond → flame diamond → 30-fluted carbide → Dialite kit.
- Three incisal designs: window/lingual-wrap, butt-joint, chamfer/incisal-overlap.
