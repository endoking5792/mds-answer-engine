# cleaning_shaping: second-reader check (2026-10-25)

Scope: all 18 canonical answers in `canonical/`.

## Method
1. **Quotation check (all answers):** every quoted phrase in a sentence ending `(Author, Year) [SRC]` was searched, case- and dash-insensitive, in the chapter files of the cited author(s) only. 121 raw hits reduced to 8 after normalising case and dashes; 4 of the 8 were parser false positives (two separate quotations in one sentence read as one). No quotation was found to come from a different book than the one cited.
2. **Number check (all exam cores):** every measurement (%, mm, μm, Hz/kHz, °, ml, rpm, N cm, minutes) in an exam-core line was searched in the books cited on that line. 3 hits, all false positives (0.8 mm and 1.5-4.0 N cm confirmed in Grossman's Ch.14, lines split across the extract).
3. **Manual read of paraphrased claims** that the scripts cannot test: table rows, lists and causes in exam cores, checked against the chapter text read for the answers (Ingle's extrusion summary, generations of NiTi, Grossman's alloy notes, Gutmann and Lovdahl on calcification, Cohen's glide path files).
4. `python3 locators.py`: 689 checked, missing 0, before and after the fixes.
