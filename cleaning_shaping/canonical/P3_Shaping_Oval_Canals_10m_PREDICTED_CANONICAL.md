## 1. Oval and irregular canals

### Explanation

Torabinejad's learning objectives include shaping canals that are "round, oval, hourglass, bowling pin, kidney bean, or ribbon" (Peters, Arias and Shabahang, 2021) [SRC]. "Oval canals extend deep into the apical area" and "apical foramina may in fact be oval in most cases" (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021) [SRC]; "a rotating file can produce a round canal at best" (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021) [SRC], leaving infected tissue in fins and isthmuses (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021) [SRC]. The first file binds to the minimum diameter (Siqueira et al, 2022) [SRC], and making the canal round "would require overenlargement" (Siqueira et al, 2022) [SRC]; 28-83% of walls may remain unprepared (Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2019) [SRC]. Strategies: "consider shaping as two canals" (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021) [SRC], two buccal and lingual paths (Peters and Arias, 2025) [SRC], brushing motion (Siqueira et al, 2022) [SRC], H-files after rotary (Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2019) [SRC], and supplementary devices (Siqueira et al, 2022) [SRC]. SAF: abrasive mesh lattice (Jain, 2016) [SRC], uniform 60-75 μm (Gopikrishna, 2021) [SRC], withdrawn (Gopikrishna, 2021) [SRC]; XP-endo Finisher spoon shape (Gopikrishna, 2021) [SRC]; TRUShape claims of up to 75% wall contact and 36% less dentine removed (Jain, 2016) [SRC]; shape-set and 3D conforming files (Peters and Arias, 2025; Peters, Arias and Shabahang, 2021) [SRC].

#### ✎ Exam core

**Introduction.** Many canals are oval, ribbon-shaped, C-shaped or joined by isthmuses, for example the distal roots of mandibular molars (Peters and Arias, 2025), and also mandibular incisors and premolars [RECALL]; the oval cross-section often extends deep into the apical area (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021).

**Problem.** Rotary files cut a round canal at best; the first file binds to the narrow diameter, so the wide buccal and lingual extensions remain untouched and filled with infected tissue and debris (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021; Siqueira et al, 2022). Even with ProTaper hand files in premolars, 28-83% of canal surfaces were left unprepared in one micro-CT study (Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2019). Enlarging to the widest diameter would overenlarge and weaken the root (Siqueira et al, 2022).

**Strategies with conventional instruments**
- Shape as two canals: buccal and lingual paths (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021; Peters and Arias, 2025).
- Brushing (lateral) strokes with rotary files against the extensions (Siqueira et al, 2022).
- Circumferential filing with stainless steel Hedström files after rotary shaping (Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2019).

**Supplementary (adaptive) instruments**

| Instrument | Principle | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Self-adjusting file (SAF) | Hollow compressible NiTi lattice; vibrating, continuous irrigation; uniform 60-75 μm removal | Adapts to cross-section; no longer marketed (Gopikrishna, 2021; Jain, 2016) |
| XP-endo Shaper / Finisher | MaxWire alloy expands at body temperature; Finisher forms a 1.5 mm spoon | Scrapes untouched walls; mainly in vitro data (Gopikrishna, 2021) |
| TRUShape | S-shaped, shape-set 3D conforming file | Up to 75% wall contact, 36% less dentine removed (manufacturer-type data in Jain, 2016) |

**Supplementary irrigation:** sonic, ultrasonic and negative-pressure systems carry irrigant into isthmuses (Siqueira et al, 2022); multisonic GentleWave also claims this (Peters, Peters and Basrani, 2021).

**Clinical sequence:** locate both extensions under magnification; shape each path with flexible NiTi to an apical size that admits the needle; brush or file towards the recesses; use an adaptive file (XP-endo Finisher) or Hedström file in the extensions; activate NaOCl and EDTA; obturate with warm vertical or hydraulic techniques that flow into irregularities (Peters and Arias, 2025; Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2019).

**Appraisal.** Adaptive and conforming files increase wall contact and conserve dentine in laboratory studies, but no clinical outcome advantage has been shown; they supplement, not replace, chemomechanical preparation with NaOCl.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Rotary = round preparation; oval canals need two paths or adaptive files.
