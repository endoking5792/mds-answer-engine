## ✎ Exam core

**Introduction.** Missed and calcified canals and separated instruments are leading technical problems in endodontics. All three involve structures below the eye's 0.2 mm limit, which is where the operating microscope with ultrasonics changes the outcome.

**1. Locating MB2 (and other extra canals):**
- MB2 detection in maxillary first molars rose from 73% before magnification to 93% with the microscope; middle mesial and middle distal canals in mandibular molars are found more often (Sikri, 2019).
- **Magnification:** 8× and 12.5× are most useful; higher power distorts the floor and loses depth (Lins et al, 2016).
- **Visual clues (Lins et al, 2016):**
  - Stropko's red or white line joining MB1 to MB2;
  - colour difference between floor and walls;
  - NaOCl bubbling over the orifice;
  - 1% methylene blue;
  - 1% sodium fluorescein with a cobalt-blue filter (green orifices).
- **Technique:** modify the access outline from triangular to trapezoidal, rectangular or rhomboidal (Lins et al, 2016); remove the dentine shelf with ultrasonic tips, whose lateral motion and slim shank keep the view clear (Sikri, 2019).

**2. Calcified canals:**
- Ultrasonic troughing of the floor under vision; the white debris marks the orifice (Gopikrishna, 2021).
- Magnification with ultrasonic troughing of the pulp chamber identifies and removes calcifications, including denticles (Sikri, 2019).
- Micro-openers and fine handpiece files negotiate the orifice.
- Seeing which walls block straight-line access allows conservative removal of dentine and avoids perforation.

**3. Separated instruments:**
- High magnification (12-20×) is used sparingly for retrieval (Gopikrishna, 2021). Work beyond the middle third needs capacity up to ×30 (Baumann and Beer, 2010).
- **Sequence:**
  1. Straight-line access and a staging platform [RECALL; see procedural_errors Q08 for the sourced technique].
  2. Thin non-diamond ultrasonic tips, touching only the fragment, loosen it.
  3. Irrigation flushes it out (Lins et al, 2016).
- The fragment, the space around it and its mobility can be seen, so dentine loss and perforation are minimised.
- Retrieval has become more predictable (Sikri, 2019).

**Conclusion.** Microscope and ultrasonics together turn blind searching into visual, conservative treatment. Training is needed, because the learning curve is long (Lins et al, 2016).

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- MB2 73% → 93%; 8× and 12.5× for searching.
- Stropko's line; fluorescein + cobalt-blue filter.
- Ultrasonics + microscope for calcifications and fragments.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES
- Searching at very high power (depth of field is lost).
- Using large burs instead of ultrasonics (perforation risk, blocked view).

## MNEMONIC
**"See-Trough-Tap"**: see at 8-12.5×, trough with ultrasonics, tap the fragment loose.
