1. **Grossman's Endodontic Practice is not cited at all, although it has a dedicated single-visit section with a named decision box.** For an Indian MDS examiner Grossman is the standard reference, and Box 16.12 (favourable vs unfavourable criteria) is the classic table.
   - Fix: add Grossman's Box 16.12 as a two-column table in the section 4 exam core, and cite Grossman in indications and contraindications. Add to the references.
   - `16_Chapter_16_Obturation_of_the_Radicular_Space.md`, PDF p.364: "Absence of periradicular" (listed among favourable criteria); "Nonsurgical retreatment cases" (contraindication); "Patients who require sedation or an operation room" (indication missing from the answer).
   - `16_Chapter_16_Obturation_of_the_Radicular_Space.md`, PDF p.363: "with the advent of rotary nickel–titanium instruments". This gives the history section its modern driver (rotary NiTi), which the answer lacks.
2. **Contraindications understate the textbook positions.**
   - Grossman lists all nonsurgical retreatment cases as a contraindication (p.364), and "presence of a periradicular lesion" plus "hot tooth" as unfavourable. Sikri lists "chronic periapical lesions of long-duration" (p.391).
   - The answer's exam core says only "symptomatic retreatment cases" and omits periapical lesions of long duration.
   - Fix: add "nonsurgical retreatment (Grossman)", "chronic periapical lesion of long duration (Sikri)" and "hot tooth" to the contraindication list. Note in the explanation that Grossman is stricter than the RCT evidence.
3. **The flare-up definition and the crown-down point are tagged [RECALL], but both are in Grossman Ch.7.** The rules say: replace [RECALL] with [SRC] where a source exists.
   - `07_Chapter_7_Endodontic_Emergencies.md`, PDF p.158: "acute exacerbation of an asymptomatic pulp or periapical pathosis" (definition). "ranges from 1.4 to 19%" (incidence, absent from the answer). "greater tendency to extrude debris" (step-back versus crown-down). "Teeth with acute alveolar abscesses have the highest" (pulpoperiapical status as a risk factor).
   - Fix: in 6A, cite Grossman for the definition, incidence, Box 7.2 predisposing factors (preoperative pain, pulpoperiapical status, shaping errors, irrigant extrusion, retreatment) and crown-down. Remove those [RECALL] tags.
4. **Flare-up management is [RECALL] where Ingle's and Cohen's give sourced points.**
   - `34_Chapter_34_Achieving_Long_Term_Success_with_Endodontic_Thera.md`, PDF p.929: "it is advisable to prescribe an appropriate antibiotic" (only when systemic signs are present).
   - `19_19_Management_of_endodontic_emergencies.md`, PDF p.2375: "trend toward not leaving teeth open for drainage". The answer should say "do not leave the tooth open".
