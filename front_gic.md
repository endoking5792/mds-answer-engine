## How to use this document
- **Each answer has two parts.** *Study Mode* explains the reasoning; *Exam Mode* is the answer-book version with an estimated writing time.
- **Citations** are in author-year form in the text, for example (Mount et al, 2016). The REFERENCES list and the SOURCE LOCATORS list at the end of each answer give the full reference, the file, the PDF page and a verbatim phrase from the source. A star (*) means the fact came from your own study notes. [RECALL] marks a statement from memory that was not verified; [Certain], [Likely] and [Guessing] mark confidence on interpretive statements.
- **Page references** are "PDF p." marker numbers of the Markdown conversions of the books.
