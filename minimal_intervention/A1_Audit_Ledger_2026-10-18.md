# A1. Audit ledger: minimally_invasive_dentistry (2026-10-18)

**Marks: n/a (reviewer ledger)**

**Scope:** 15 new answers (11 repository questions Q1-Q11 and 4 predicted P1-P4) on minimally invasive / minimal intervention dentistry (MID), drafted from the textbooks in the private repo `endoking5792/mds-textbooks` and checked against them. Questions come from the Conservative Dentistry master repository (section "Minimally invasive / minimal intervention dentistry", 27 printed wordings over 25 sittings) and the Recent Advances repository; equivalent wordings are answered once at their highest marks. "Discuss MID, ideal materials and GICs" (20m) is already answered as Glass Ionomer Cement Q9 and is not repeated.

## Method
- **Source reading.** Four readers built fact dossiers with page locators from the books (Pickard's Guide to Minimally Invasive Operative Dentistry; Mount and Hume; Sikri; the preclinical texts; Summitt; Sturdevant 2nd South Asia edition; Modern Operative Dentistry; the materials and caries books). A fifth reader checked guidelines and key papers by web search (Europe PMC abstracts; PubMed and PMC were blocked).
- **Drafting.** Five writers drafted the answers to the length ceilings in the skill (Section 7.1), citing author-year with a source-locator line (book, PDF page, verbatim phrase) for each fact.
- **Verification.** Four independent readers then checked every cited sentence, bullet and table cell against the cited chapter. The findings were verified again in the books before being applied.
- **Mechanical checks.** Every locator (phrase and PDF page marker) was searched in the `mds-textbooks` files; word counts, section structure and line endings were checked.
- **Rule used (from the user):** where textbooks disagree, name each author, edition and year and show what each says side by side. No source is ranked.

## Result
- **Locators:** 987 locator lines checked against the books; 987 match the stated PDF page and phrase exactly.
- **Claims:** about 1,290 cited claims were checked by the verification readers; about 150 problems were logged (wrong attributions, one-sided comparisons, omitted caveats, one factual error about the Hall technique, internal inconsistencies, unsourced web-page citations). Every finding that held up in the book was applied; a few were left out for space or because the passage could not be found, and are listed in each answer's log.
- **Length:** Exam Mode word counts are inside the approved range for every answer.

## Points the sources do not settle (as stated in the answers)
- **"Golden triangle"** (Q4): the term appears in the question but is not defined in any of the textbook files read or in an authoritative web source that could be confirmed. Q4 says so and gives a labelled synthesis from what the books state (Pickard's care cycle; Sikri's components); it does not present a definition.
- **Tunnel restorations:** Mount, Pickard, Sikri, Summitt and Sturdevant are shown side by side (Mount favourable; Summitt "should be avoided").
- **Silver diamine fluoride:** about 30% (Craig) and 38% (Sikri; Sturdevant 2nd South Asia edition); the risk-of-bias statement comes from one meta-analysis quoted by Hill and Gillam.
- **Carisolv:** 0.5% (Sikri) and 0.1% (Pickard).
- **Selective removal in deep lesions:** terminology and recommendations differ between the books and are shown separately.

## Limits
- **Thin sources:** the Hall technique (Sikri; Kidd and Fejerskov), interim therapeutic restoration (Summitt only) and the FDI principles (Sikri p.554) rest on one or two books each; the answers mark the evidence level.
- **Web items** (Tyas 2000, Frencken 2012, Pitts and Ekstrand 2013, Schwendicke 2016, Innes 2017, Slayton 2018) were read as abstracts only. The 2019 consensus update, the Cochrane review of the Hall technique and the Banerjee 2013 paper could not be found and are not used. A FDI web page was read once without a recorded URL or year, so it was removed.
- **Journal and web claims** were not checked against full texts. The repository's Markdown copy of the questions was read; it was not diffed against the Drive Word files.
- Reader reports are model-written. Each fix was verified in the book by the patch writer; the "risk assessment is inevitably a best guess" sentence was re-read by hand (Summitt pp.267-268). Not every edit was re-read by a person.
