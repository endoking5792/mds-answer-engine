# Newer classification of dental caries

**Marks:** 10 · Short essay

**Answer blueprint:** Introduction → Criteria-based classification → G.V. Black and its limits → Mount and Hume site and size → Visual scoring systems → Radiographic classification → Conclusion.

## Introduction

Newer classifications describe the **lesion** (site, severity, activity) rather than the restoration, so that early lesions can be recorded, monitored and managed without cutting.

## Criteria-based classification

| Criterion | Types |
|---|---|
| Site | Pit and fissure, smooth surface, root, linear enamel caries (Sikri, 2020) |
| Rate | Acute, chronic (Singh, 2020); rampant: ten or more new lesions a year; arrested (Sikri, 2020) |
| Extent | Incipient (reversible), cavitated (non-reversible) (Singh, 2020) |
| Spread | Forward, backward; hidden (occult) (Singh, 2020) |
| Restoration | Primary; secondary, now CARS (caries adjacent to a restoration or sealant); residual (Longbottom and Ferreira Zandona, 2019) |
| Age and cause | Nursing bottle, adolescent, geriatric; radiation caries (Sikri, 2020) |

## G.V. Black and its limits

Classes I-V, with Class VI added by Simon (Singh, 2020). Black described the **final restoration location, not the lesion**, and could not record lesion size, progress or activity (Mount et al., 2016).

## Mount and Hume site and size (1997)

- **Site:** 1 pit/fissure, 2 approximal, 3 cervical or exposed root.
- **Size:** 0 earliest demineralisation, no cavity (noted P, N or R: progressing, non-progressing, reversed); 1 minimal cavitation, just beyond remineralisation; 2 moderate; 3 enlarged; 4 extensive (Mount et al., 2016). Thus 2.1 is a minimal approximal cavity.

Books differ: Singh (2020) calls size 3 "advanced"; Sikri (2020) dates the system 1998, omits size 0 and treats size 1 "by remineralization alone", contradicting Mount's own text.

## Visual scoring systems

| System | Basis |
|---|---|
| ICDAS | Two-digit code: restoration status, then caries 0-6, grouped initial (1-2), moderate (3-4), extensive (5-6) (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019); ICCMS merges the codes into four (Mount et al., 2016) |
| Nyvad | Activity (active/inactive) and severity (non-cavitated/cavitated), from surface features (Machiulskiene, 2019) |
| ADA CCS | Sound, initial, moderate, advanced (Zeller, Young and Novy, 2019) |

ICDAS dates differ: a 2002 Dundee conference (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019; Mount et al., 2016), a 2004 Foundation (Banerjee and Watson, 2011) and 2009 (Singh, 2020); the 2002 origin is best documented.

## Radiographic classification

E1, E2 (outer, inner enamel half), D1-D3 (outer, middle, inner dentine third) (Schwendicke and Göstemeyer, 2019).

## Conclusion

Newer systems link lesion stage and activity to non-operative or operative care.
