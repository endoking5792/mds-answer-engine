# ICDAS II and ICCMS: detection codes, activity assessment and the risk-based management pathway

**Marks:** 10 · Short Essay (PREDICTED)

**Answer blueprint:** Introduction → ICDAS II codes → activity assessment → ICCMS staging → risk-based management → evaluation → conclusion.

## Introduction

**ICDAS** is a histologically validated visual system staging caries severity and activity. Dates differ: Dundee conference 2002, activity and radiographic criteria 2005 and 2007 (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019); ICDAS 2003, **ICDAS II** 2005 (Caneppele et al., 2020); Foundation convened 2004 (Banerjee and Watson, 2015).

## ICDAS II detection codes

On **clean, dry** teeth with a ball-ended probe, each surface gets **two digits**: restoration/sealant status (0-9), then caries severity (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019; Caneppele et al., 2020). Boushell and Shugars (2019) reverse the order (severity first); follow the developers' order.

| Code | Sign | Histology (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | sound | sound (95%) |
| 1 | first visual change, seen after air drying | none or outer half of enamel (95%) |
| 2 | distinct change, seen wet | inner enamel or outer dentine third (83%) |
| 3 | localised enamel breakdown | middle dentine third (67%) |
| 4 | underlying dark dentine shadow | as code 3 |
| 5 | distinct cavity, dentine visible | inner dentine third (84%) |
| 6 | extensive cavity | as code 5 |

Amaechi et al. (2013) place code 2 through the whole enamel into outer dentine. Pickard's **mICDAS** scores 0-4 (Banerjee and Watson, 2015).

## Activity assessment

Codes 3-6 are probably active. For codes 1-2, points for **location**, **appearance**, **texture** and **gingival bleeding** are summed; more than 7 means active, cavitation, roughness and soft dentine weighing most (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019).

## ICCMS

The seven codes merge into four stages, **sound, initial (1-2), moderate (3-4) and extensive (5-6)** (Mount et al., 2016; Ekstrand and Braga, 2019); radiographs are scored RA1-RA3, RB4 and RC5-RC6 (Zeller, Young and Novy, 2019).

**Diagram:** risk + stage + activity → management → recall.

## Risk-based management pathway

ICCMS links **lesion stage and activity**, **patient risk** and **risk-based recall** (Caneppele et al., 2020):

- **Arrested:** treat only for aesthetics or function.
- **Active initial lesions:** fluoride, hygiene training, sealing or infiltration, silver diamine fluoride.
- **Moderate/extensive:** sealing, restoration, stepwise excavation (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019; Fontana and Huysmans, 2019).

## Evaluation

Kappa 0.67 or above, sensitivity and specificity 0.7 (Ekstrand and Braga, 2019); but hard to chart clinically (Kidd and Fejerskov, 2016).

## Conclusion

ICCMS turns reproducible ICDAS II codes and patient risk into preventive, tooth-preserving care.
