# Quantitative light induced fluorescence (QLF)

**Marks:** 10 · Short essay

**Answer blueprint:** Definition → principle → parameters → devices → technique → applications → limitations → clinical role → conclusion.

## Definition

**QLF** is a non-destructive optical method for **early detection and quantification** of caries, measuring **mineral loss as fluorescence loss** and **bacterial activity as red fluorescence** simultaneously (Kim, 2019). Introduced in 1981 using 488 nm blue (argon) laser light, it was dentistry's first optical technique after X-rays (Kim, 2019; Muruppel and Fried, 2023). Pickard's table calls it "quantitative laser fluorescence" (Banerjee and Watson, 2011), but it is light-induced, unlike DIAGNOdent.

## Principle

1. Blue light excites DEJ fluorophores, which glow green and back-illuminate enamel (Kim, 2019; Caneppele et al., 2020).
2. A porous white spot scatters light **5-10 times** more than sound enamel, so it appears **dark** (Kim, 2019).
3. A high-pass filter (above 520 nm) removes excitation light (Muruppel and Fried, 2023).
4. Porphyrins in mature plaque (at least 3 days old) and active lesions fluoresce **red** (Kim, 2019).

**Diagram:** blue light in, green dentine glow, dark lesion, red plaque, filter, camera.

## Parameters (software)

**ΔF** (%) = lesion depth (r = 0.88 with microradiography); **ΔQ** (mm²%) = lesion volume; **ΔR** (%) = bacterial activity (Kim, 2019).

## Devices: books differ on the light source

| Source | Light |
|---|---|
| Kim (2019) | 405 nm blue; Inspektor, QLF-D, Qray devices |
| Muruppel and Fried (2023) | xenon arc lamp, 370 nm |
| Sikri (2020) | 50 W xenon arc lamp, 290-450 nm |

## Technique

Clean teeth first; image occlusal, buccal and lingual aspects (Kim, 2019).

## Applications (Kim, 2019)

- **Occlusal:** aids ICDAS codes 1-2; red fluorescence separates caries from extrinsic stain.
- **Proximal:** sensitivity 71% (enamel) and 64% (dentine); specificity 84% and 88%.
- **Smooth surface:** ΔF of -14.6 or less: at least 50% recover with fluoride.
- **Root caries**, **secondary caries**, **sealant failure**; orthodontic demineralisation (Sikri, 2020).

## Limitations: books differ

| Point | Kim (2019) | Muruppel and Fried (2023) | Sikri (2020) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dentine lesions | detected | not detected | not stated |
| Proximal lesions | detected | not stated | not detected |

Sensitivity 64-94%, specificity 84-100%; affected by ambient light (Kim, 2019). It cannot separate caries from hypoplasia (Muruppel and Fried, 2023).

## Clinical role: books differ

Kim (2019): now available for clinical dentistry. Kidd and Fejerskov (2016): fluorescent tools "only have research interest", not guiding treatment decisions.

## Conclusion

QLF quantifies and monitors early lesions without radiation but supplements visual examination.
