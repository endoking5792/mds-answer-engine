The diagram cue and conclusion are synthesis.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) dates: Ekstrand and Braga (Dundee 2002; activity and radiographic criteria 2005, 2007) vs Caneppele et al. (ICDAS 2003, ICDAS II 2005) vs Mount et al. (developed 2002) — shown. (2) code 2 histology: Ekstrand and Braga 83% inner half of enamel or outer third dentine vs Amaechi et al. entire enamel plus outer third of dentine — shown; Amaechi et al. also give 5-6 as pulpal third (consistent with Ekstrand's inner third). (3) internal inconsistency in Ekstrand and Braga: text says arrested ≤7, active >7 points; the Fig. 6.3 legend says 7 or more is active (p1_fig7 locates it, PDF 54). The answer follows the text and the figure's own panel labels (">7 points" active, "≤ 7 points" arrested); not shown in the answer for length. (4) the simplified four-stage format is called ICCS by Amaechi et al. (Summitt) and ICCMS by Mount et al.; Ekstrand and Braga give the three merged diagnoses initial/moderate/extensive. Answer uses ICCMS wording. (5) Kidd and Fejerskov prefer the Nyvad criteria and find ICDAS hard to chart; Ekstrand and Braga call it robust — shown briefly.
