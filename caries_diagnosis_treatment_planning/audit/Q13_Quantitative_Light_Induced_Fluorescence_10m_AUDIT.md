## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the numbered principle sequence is a teaching arrangement of Kim's and Caneppele et al.'s descriptions; the diagram cue and the conclusion sentence are uncited synthesis. "Mature plaque and active lesions contain porphyrins" combines Kim's plaque statement (porphyrin-containing biofilm, at least 3 days old) with his active-lesion red fluorescence finding.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) light source: Kim 405 nm (Inspektor Pro; his Table 16.1 also lists a halogen lamp for the first generation) vs Muruppel and Fried xenon/arc lamp at 370 nm (range 290-488) vs Sikri 50 W xenon arc lamp 290-450 nm; original 488 nm argon laser in both Kim and Muruppel and Fried. (2) dentine lesions: Kim's summary table and Ko et al. data (64% dentine sensitivity) say yes; Muruppel and Fried say QLF cannot detect lesions extending into dentin. (3) proximal: Kim reports QLF-D proximal detection; Sikri lists inability to detect/monitor interproximal lesions. (4) Muruppel and Fried date the technique to Bjelkhagen and Sundström 1981 and de Jong 1995; Kim's reference for the principle is 1982 but he dates introduction to 1981 (consistent).
