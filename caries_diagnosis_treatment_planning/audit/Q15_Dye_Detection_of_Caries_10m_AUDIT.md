## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** "complete stain-free excavation is no longer the aim" is reasoning from Maltz et al.'s point that sealing carious dentine leaves less infection than complete removal guided by dye; written as a consequence, not as a quoted recommendation. "Rinse with water" is from the same technique sentence (q15_drop context). Conclusion is synthesis.
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) value of dyes: Hilton and Summitt cite studies showing benefit in identifying and removing demineralised dentine and reducing viable bacteria; Maltz et al. conclude dye staining is a poor indicator, with clinical sensitivity 65.1% and specificity 37.6% (Peskersoy et al.), and no good-quality evidence. (2) bonding: Hilton and Summitt "preponderance of evidence" no concern; Maltz et al. say the effect remains unclear (in vitro data only, conflicting). Shown in a table. Both agree on false staining of deep sound dentine and pulp-exposure risk. (3) Layer description: Sikri describes the inner layer as "decalcified ... hard" and remineralisable, broadly consistent with Maltz et al.'s affected dentine.
- **Numerical claims:** 0.5% fuchsin (q15_fuch); 1% acid red (q15_shim, q15_cd); acid red 52 (q15_cd); 1% FD&C green (q15_cfg); 5 s / 10 s (q15_drop, q15_10s); 65.1%, 37.6% (q15_pesk); 70% (q15_70); ZL-22 (q15_sk_zyglo); 1970s: Maltz et al. write "early 1970s" in the sentence containing q15_fuch (PDF 86) — the decade is not inside the locator phrase.
