## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **Conflicting sources:** distance from the DEJ (Sturdevant 0.5-1 mm p.384; Summitt 0.5 mm minimum, 1.0 mm preferable; Marzouk 1.5-2.0 mm for threaded pins p.209), pinhole depth (Sturdevant 2 mm p.383; Summitt/Dilts 2-3 mm), minimum interpin distance (Sturdevant 3/5 mm p.385; Marzouk 4 mm p.207). Shown as a table. The explanation that Marzouk's figures aim to avoid enamel crazing follows from Marzouk's own context (crazing section) and is worded as reasoning.
- **Interpretation:** 'young vital dentin holds best' paraphrases Marzouk's dentin-type factor.
- **Technique:** pilot-hole bur, gingival-crevice alignment and 90-degree check are Sturdevant pp.386-387; the locators cover rpm, number of movements, the reverse turn and the 2 mm cut-off.
