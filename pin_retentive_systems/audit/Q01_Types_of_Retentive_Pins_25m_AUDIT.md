## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **Conflicting sources:** self-threading vs cemented retention, 3-6x (Ritter et al, Sturdevant's p.382) vs 5-6x (Marzouk p.208); shown side by side in the answer. The reason offered (test conditions) is interpretation, labelled as such.
- **Conflicting sources (not in this answer):** Bhat et al list 'silver retentive pins' among amalgam alloy uses (Science of Dental Materials, direct filling alloys), while Marzouk says all-silver pins are not effective; left out of Q01 because Bhat gives no context.
- **Numerical claims:** TMS diameters and colours from Sturdevant Table 14.1 (p.382); channel mismatches from Sturdevant p.382; 40% varnish loss Marzouk p.209.
- **Interpretation:** the closing 'balance retention against stress' sentence is synthesis.
