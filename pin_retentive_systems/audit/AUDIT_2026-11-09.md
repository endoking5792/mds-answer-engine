## Conflicts between books (shown side by side in the answers)
- Self-threading vs cemented retention: 3-6 times (Sturdevant) vs 5-6 times (Marzouk; also Modern Operative Dentistry) — Q01.
- Distance from DEJ: 0.5-1 mm (Sturdevant), at least 0.5 mm with 1.0 mm preferable (Summitt), 1.5-2.0 mm for threaded pins (Marzouk) — Q02.
- Pinhole depth: 2 mm (Sturdevant) vs 2-3 mm (Summitt, from Dilts) — Q02.
- Minimum interpin distance: 3 mm Minikin / 5 mm Minim (Sturdevant) vs 4 mm small threaded (Marzouk) — Q02.
- Pins vs bonding: greater retention with pins (Sturdevant) vs equal clinical success (Summitt) — Q03, reconciled as different measures.
- Silver pins: listed as a use by Bhat, called ineffective by Marzouk — sidecar only.
