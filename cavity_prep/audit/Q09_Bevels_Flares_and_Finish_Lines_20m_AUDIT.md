## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **Classification:** The six bevel categories follow the Marzouk/Singh scheme. Other authors differ in whether hollow-ground, reverse and inverted bevels are treated as separate categories. The answer signals that terminology is not universal.
- **Angles:** The migrated answer retains Sturdevant’s cast-metal marginal-angle targets. The older answer mixed this with angles measured against the crown long axis or external tooth surface. Those measurements should not be compared as though they share the same reference line.
- **Marginal fit:** Fan et al.’s cement-thickness discussion is described as laboratory/geometric evidence, not clinical outcome evidence. The answer avoids claiming that a bevel invariably improves marginal fit.
- **Material-specific indications:** The answer distinguishes cast-metal designs from amalgam, glass-ionomer and ceramic margins. Posterior composite occlusal beveling is not recommended in the cited chapter; this should not be overgeneralised to all anterior enamel beveling.
- **Bibliography:** Marzouk’s publication year remains unknown in the extracted source copy. Do not infer a year from other editions or reprints.
