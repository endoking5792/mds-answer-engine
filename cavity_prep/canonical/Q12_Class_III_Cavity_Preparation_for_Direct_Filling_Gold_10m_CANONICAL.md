## Explanation

**Indications and principles (Sturdevant's).** Class III direct gold suits proximal anterior lesions small enough for an esthetic result (Smith, 2019) [SRC]; contraindications include very large pulp chambers (Smith, 2019) [SRC] and root-filled teeth (Smith, 2019) [SRC]. Outline must be esthetically designed (Smith, 2019) [SRC]; all enamel supported by dentin (Smith, 2019) [SRC]; retention by parallelism of some walls and converging walls (Smith, 2019) [SRC]; internal line angles sharp to resist movement (Smith, 2019) [SRC]. Convenience: rubber dam (Smith, 2019) [SRC] and a separator giving at most 0.5 mm separation for Class III (Smith, 2019) [SRC].

**Three designs (Marzouk).** Marzouk describes three basic Class III designs (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC].
- *Ferrier:* indicated when bulky labial, lingual and incisal walls remain after caries removal (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; more for distal than mesial surfaces (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; needs a skilful operator (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]. Triangular outline (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC] involving about two-thirds to one-half of the proximal surface (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; labial margin minimally extended in the embrasure (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; lingual margin must not encroach on the marginal ridge (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; gingival margin slightly subgingival (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; incisal margin usually in the contact area (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]. Boxed incisal retention penetrating (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC] dentin 1.5 mm (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; pyramidal retention at the gingival point angles (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; all line and point angles sharp (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC] because the design is mainly for cohesive gold foil (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; modified for delicate lower incisors (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC] with very limited labial extent (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC] and an exceptionally flat axial wall (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]. Instrumentation starts with a No. 2 round bur in lateral cutting strokes (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; triangles are cut with a Wedelstaedt chisel in direct cutting strokes (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; walls are formed with the base of an inverted cone bur (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; a bibevelled hatchet boxes the incisal retention (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]. The angle former places the short partial enamel bevel gingivally (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC].
- *Loma Linda:* for a powdered-gold build-up with a foil veneer (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC], lingual access (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC], the lingual margin far enough onto the lingual surface to include the marginal ridge (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; proximal part triangular with rounded corners (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; gingival retention grooves about 1.5-2 mm into dentin (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; angles more rounded than Ferrier (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC].
- *Ingraham:* for incipient proximal lesions where esthetics dominates (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC], needing good hygiene and low caries indices (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; a simple parallelogram (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; labial margin in the contact area, "truly invisible labially" (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC], lingual margin past the marginal ridge (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; triangular retention areas in the incisal and gingival walls (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC].
- Instruments: spear bur made by sharpening a bur neck (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC]; Wedelstaedt chisel to cut the labial-wall triangle (Marzouk et al, undated) [SRC].

## ✎ Exam core

**Introduction.** Direct filling gold (cohesive foil, powdered gold) is compacted into the cavity, so the preparation must provide sharp, definite internal form, mechanical retention and walls to condense against.

**Indications:** small proximal lesions in anterior teeth with good hygiene; contraindicated with large pulps and root-filled teeth (Smith, 2019).

**Principles (Smith, 2019)**
- Outline: smooth, in sound, finishable areas, esthetically placed.
- Resistance: enamel supported by dentin.
- Retention: parallel and converging walls, sharp internal line angles, point retentions.
- Convenience: rubber dam; separator (maximum 0.5 mm).

**Designs (Marzouk et al, undated)**

| | Ferrier | Loma Linda | Ingraham |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indication | Bulky walls remain; skilled operator; distal surfaces | Lingual access, lost/undermined marginal ridge | Incipient lesions, esthetics paramount, low caries risk |
| Material | Cohesive foil | Powdered gold + foil veneer | Cohesive foil |
| Outline | Triangular, half to two-thirds of proximal surface | Triangular, rounded corners, lingual extension | Parallelogram |
| Labial margin | In labial embrasure, minimal | Contact area or embrasure | In contact area, invisible |
| Lingual margin | Must not involve marginal ridge | Includes marginal ridge | Past the marginal ridge |
| Retention | Incisal box 1.5 mm; gingival pyramidal point retentions | Gingival grooves 1.5-2 mm; incisal retention | Triangular incisal and gingival retention |
| Angles | Sharp | Rounded | Flat axial wall |

**Steps (Ferrier):** separation and dam; outline with No. 2 round bur; walls formed with an inverted cone bur; triangles cut with Wedelstaedt chisel and angle former; point retentions with spear bur; incisal box with bibevelled hatchet; short partial enamel bevel on margins with angle former/chisel (Marzouk et al, undated).

**Modification for mandibular incisors:** minimal labial extent, flat axial wall, two-plane lingual wall (Marzouk et al, undated).

**Conclusion.** The design chosen depends on access, esthetics, lesion size and the gold form: Ferrier for foil with sharp angles, Loma Linda for powdered gold from lingual, Ingraham for small invisible lesions.

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Three designs: Ferrier, Loma Linda, Ingraham (Marzouk).
- Separation no more than 0.5 mm (Smith, 2019).
- Ferrier incisal box 1.5 mm; Loma Linda grooves 1.5-2 mm.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES
- Using amalgam/composite Class III outlines for gold.
- Forgetting separation and point retentions.
- Placing the Ferrier lingual margin on the marginal ridge.

## MNEMONIC
**"FLI"**: Ferrier (foil, sharp), Loma Linda (lingual, powdered), Ingraham (incipient, invisible).
