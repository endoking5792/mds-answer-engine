# Factors that affect the accuracy of electronic apex locators and how to overcome them.

**Marks:** 10 · Short essay (predicted)

**Answer blueprint:** Introduction → Factors and remedies → Where books differ → Verification → Conclusion.

## Introduction

A locator reads impedance between file and mucosa, so an extra conducting path or a break in the circuit falsifies it. A **false positive** ("apex" shown early) comes from a short circuit: metal restorations, gingival overgrowth, parapulpal pins, root fractures, lateral or false canals (Baumann and Beer, 2010). A **false negative** (no signal at the apex) comes from calcified canals or apical blockage by dentine chips (Hülsmann and Schäfer, 2009).

## Factors and remedies

| Factor | Effect | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| File touching a metal restoration or crown; metal stopper | False short reading (Gopikrishna, 2021) | Remove or isolate with a plastic capillary tip (Munce et al, 2019); varnish the shaft (Jain, 2016); silicone stopper (Baumann and Beer, 2010) |
| Fluid or saliva in chamber; loose lip clip | Short circuit | Dry the chamber with a cotton pellet (Baumann and Beer, 2010); check the clip (Weine, 2004) |
| Blood, exudate, remnant pulp | Inaccurate readings (Jain, 2016) | Remove tissue; dry a wet canal or moisten a dry one (Weine, 2004) |
| Open, immature or resorbed apex | Short readings (Jain, 2016); unreliable beyond a size 100 K-file (Malkhassian, Plazas and Nahmias, 2012) | Snug-fitting file (Gutmann and Lovdahl, 2011); add other methods (Jain, 2016) |
| Debris, calcification, lost patency | False negative | Patency, recapitulation and irrigation (Jain, 2016) |
| Old gutta-percha | Insulates (Sikri, 2019) | Remove it; a clean file regains accuracy (Roda et al, 2021) |
| Low battery, no calibration, inexperience | Errors (Jain, 2016; Weine, 2004) | Check battery (poor below 50%), calibrate |
| Device generation | First two generations failed with conductive fluids (Malkhassian, Plazas and Nahmias, 2012); fourth needs a relatively dry canal, fifth struggles in dry ones (Jain, 2016) | Match moisture to the unit |

Pulp vitality (Malkhassian, Plazas and Nahmias, 2012; Peters et al, 2021) and file alloy (Jain, 2016) do not matter.

## Where books differ

| Issue | Position |
|---|---|
| File size | Accuracy appears unaffected by canal or file size (Malkhassian, Plazas and Nahmias, 2012); small files reduce reliability (Jain, 2016) |
| Preflaring | May increase accuracy (Malkhassian, Plazas and Nahmias, 2012); does not seem to affect it (Jain, 2016) |

## Verification

Confirm radiographically (Gulabivala and Ng, 2014); if readings vary, repeat and radiograph (Jain, 2016).

## Conclusion

Most errors are short or broken circuits, preventable by a dry chamber, insulated file, patent canal and snug file, with radiographic confirmation.
