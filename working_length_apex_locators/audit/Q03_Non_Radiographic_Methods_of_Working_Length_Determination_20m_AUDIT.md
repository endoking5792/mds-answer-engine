## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator. Interpretation only: 'the older methods are too unreliable to be the main technique' generalises Malkhassian et al.'s statement (which covers all five methods they list) to the four non-radiographic ones; Walton, Gutmann, Gulabivala and Attin independently say the same for patient response, tactile sense and paper point. Gopikrishna (2021) was dropped from the final text for length (it allows EAL alone but recommends both); its locators q03_gro_alone and q03_gro_combo remain available.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the table 'Key point' column for the first, third and sixth generations is a compressed reading of Sikri's Table 15.1 and text. The Comparison table (merit/limitation) condenses the sections above it. The four-step combined protocol joins Gulabivala and Ng (EAL, then radiograph, triangulate), Jain (which length to trust) and Gutmann (flare first).
- **Conflicting sources:** (1) Generations: Sikri numbers a fourth 'modified frequency' (Raypex 4, ProPex) and a fifth 'Bluetooth' (Raypex 5) type; Munce et al. number a fourth (Elements, database comparison, dry canal) and a fifth (separate capacitance and resistance, 2003) type; both end with an adaptive sixth. (2) Pacemakers: Teixeira et al. advise avoiding the devices; Munce et al. call the concern unnecessary; Malkhassian et al., Cohen and the Hulsmann-Schafer text advise consulting the physician or cardiologist. (3) Accuracy: Sikri 90%, Weine 'low 90s', Walton 80-95% (impedance type), Munce 81.5-97% clinical and 90.4% vs 82.1% RCT, Harty >90% within 0.5 mm, Barnett 100% vs 15% (one in vivo study of 482 canals). Malkhassian et al. also print 'between 8% and 94%' in one sentence (probably a typographical slip for 80%; not used). (4) Weight given to EAL: Gutmann and Lovdahl 'serious consideration as primary means'; Weine 'never eliminate radiographs'; Gopikrishna 'can be used alone, but combined use recommended'. (5) Resistance of periodontium to mucosa: 6.5 kilo-ohms (Sikri, Munce, Gutmann, Weine, Jain) versus 6 kilo-ohms (Attin and Hoer): rounding, not a real conflict. (6) Pull-back: Malkhassian et al. pass the foramen then withdraw to the constriction; Peters and Arias withdraw about 0.5 mm from patency; Gulabivala and Ng use the 'zero' reading itself. (7) Textbook slip: the 1918 pioneer is printed 'Cluster' in Ingle's 7e (Munce et al.), Grossman's 14e and Jain, but 'Custer' in Malkhassian et al. and in Cohen's reference list; the answer avoids the name.
- **Numerical claims:** 19-25 mm, crown ~10 mm, root 9-15 mm: Cohen Ch.8 (q03_coh_len, q03_coh_crown). Constriction 0-4 mm, average 1 mm: Attin and Hoer. Tactile helpful <60%: Malkhassian et al.
