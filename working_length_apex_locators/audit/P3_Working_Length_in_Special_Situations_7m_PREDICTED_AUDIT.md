## Evidence and uncertainty notes

- **[RECALL] / unverified:** none stated as fact without a locator.
- **Interpretation / synthesis:** the table collects one statement per situation from the books named; 'Flare first' compresses Gutmann and Lovdahl's advice that a more accurate length follows straight-line access (p3_gut_straight). - **Conflicting sources:** (1) Resorption and locators: Gutmann and Lovdahl say locators are unreliable and of little value when the root end is wide open from resorption; Cohen (Peters, Peters, Basrani) says apical resorption does not significantly affect locator accuracy; Jain reports Root ZX read 'apex' even without a constriction in simulated resorption. (2) Resorption shortening: Weine 0.5 mm, up to 2 mm; Walton and Torabinejad 1 / 1.5 / 2 mm by bone and root findings; Gopikrishna (Weine's modification) 1.5 mm for bone resorption and 2 mm for root resorption; Sikri 0.5 mm, 2 mm; Gutmann and Lovdahl 1-2 mm coronal to a sound point; Gulabivala and Ng record two schools (shorter vs full length with vigorous preparation). (3) Open apex: Jain says locators are of little use; Cohen says accuracy improves once the foramen narrows; Ingle 7e (Munce et al.) lists immature apices among conditions that reduce accuracy; Gopikrishna Ch.11 gives 2 mm short of the radiographic apex for apexification.
