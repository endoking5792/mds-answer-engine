## Checked and held up (selected)
- 87.3%: Gulabivala PDF 298, "in 87.3% of teeth associated with [periradicular periodontitis]" (sentence runs onto the next column). This is Gulabivala's figure, correctly attributed. Levin "invariably" (PDF 727) and Sikri "practically all" (PDF 512) are quoted accurately. "Differ in emphasis" is fair, not a conflict.
- 38% after intrusion (Andreasen and Vestergaard Pedersen) and 60% after extrusion (Crona-Larsson et al) are both in Patel et al's chapter (Cohen's Ch.18 PDF 2312), not in Ingle's or Andreasen's own book. 5-18% and 30% are on the same page.
- 66%-72% cervical fracture with calcium hydroxide beyond 30 days in immature teeth: Ingle's PDF 379, "two clinical studies".
- 7-10 days closed apex: Patel et al PDF 2316 (replanted teeth with closed apices, "even if there are no radiographic signs"); Kahler PDF 2644 (extraoral time under 60 min, closed apex). Open apex, 3-4 week recall, apexification at the first sign: Kahler PDF 2645.
- Kahler: 1-month dense refill and 3-month radiographs (PDF 2621) belong to the late-start or active-EIR protocol, so they sit correctly in the "Established EIR" row. Change no more than every 3 months (PDF 2644).
- Harty's 6-10 years (PDF 267) and 6-24 months (PDF 273) are in context.
- Onset: Ingle's PDF 378 "2–4 weeks"; Patel 2 weeks (PDF 2312), 3-4 weeks and within 1 year (PDF 2315).
- Q04: Levin 7 days of calcium hydroxide, dentin shelf, apical surgery and crown-root ratio (PDF 728); halt orthodontics 2-3 months (PDF 726); AAE constriction 0.5-1.0 mm (Glossary PDF 6); Baumann and Beer pressure ceases (PDF 352); Ingle's 9-10 mm (PDF 378; context is repair-related surface resorption, but the sentence covers any resorbed apex); transient apical breakdown as luxation healing (Ingle's PDF 375, AAE PDF 45) vs orthodontic (Sikri PDF 516). That disagreement is real and fairly shown.
