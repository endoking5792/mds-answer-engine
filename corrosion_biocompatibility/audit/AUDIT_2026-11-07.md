## 3. Conflicts between books (shown side by side in the answers)
1. **Sign convention** (C1): Phillips'/McCabe use reduction potentials (gold +1.50 V; more negative corrodes). Craig's 14th Table 4.16 uses oxidation potentials (gold −1.498 V) and says large negative = more tarnish resistant. Same ranking, opposite sign. Not an error; students must state the convention.
2. **Which amalgam is the anode** (C1, P1): Phillips' — high-copper is cathodic to conventional; Craig's galvanic series in saliva agrees. Bhat et al — high-copper becomes the anode. Followed Phillips'/Craig's [Likely]; Bhat et al probably in error.
3. **Insulating base against galvanic shock** (P1): Bhat et al recommend one; Craig's — a cement base does not effectively insulate the pulp; Phillips' uses varnish (insulates from saliva). Followed Craig's/Phillips'.
4. **Gold electroplating** (P2): Bhat et al — prevents inhomogeneous-surface corrosion; Phillips' — coatings generally ineffective, a scratched gold coat accelerates base-metal attack. Reconciled: protects only while intact.
5. **Ames test "validated"** (C2): Craig's 14th — the only thoroughly validated short-term test; Craig's 11th — only 45% sensitive. Reconciled in the critical synthesis.
6. **ISO 10993 parts** (C2, C3): 12 (2002) vs 20 (2010) — dates differ, not a conflict.
7. **Where animal usage tests sit** (C2): McCabe level 2 vs Craig's "usage tests". Terminology only.
